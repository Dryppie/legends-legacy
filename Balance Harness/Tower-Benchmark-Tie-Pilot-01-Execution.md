## Prospective decision and all-root results

The original decision rule is unchanged: first abandon if either mean is at or below −2 points; otherwise report no observed output differentiation when no roots differ; otherwise require at least +2 method points, a nonnegative benchmark mean and at least three differing roots for a larger fresh evaluation. Novelty counts are descriptive and do not affect this selector decision. The saved result and an independent arithmetic recount agree on **AbandonThisConfiguration**. **The benchmark mean crosses the −2-point abandonment threshold.** A positive comparison with the control alone does not establish improvement over retaining the stronger benchmark.

This is a prospective development decision for this exact configuration. It does not establish that the tie override is generally harmful or that affinity creation cannot work. Uncertainty about future roots remains; no decision threshold or interval was changed after observing these results.
