## Fixed measurement and uncertainty

After separate admission, one OS CSPRNG batch of **65,536 bytes /16,384 signed Int32 words** supplies the first **2,048 distinct values absent from the complete pre-draw history**. Every exposed fresh value is permanently reserved, including unused values. Collisions and duplicates remain recorded. There is no refill or redraw. Hashes bind the evidence but cannot establish entropy provenance alone.

All 44 teams receive the same ordered panel. A shared physical recipe is evaluated once and contributes to both neighborhood summaries. Draws count as non-wins. Historical measurements are not reused as fresh observations, and there is no racing, adaptive nomination or validation-gate replay.

The **46 fixed linear endpoints** are:

- 43 nonbenchmark teams' win-rate gains against the fixed benchmark, including both other references;
- the equal-recipe mean gain of each complete generated neighborhood against the benchmark;
- the protected-neighborhood mean minus the full-neighborhood mean.

Each endpoint is a fixed linear combination of per-value binary wins. The design applies a two-sided Hoeffding bound and a union bound over all 46 endpoints, with family alpha **0.05**. For range `[a,b]`, half-width is `(b-a) × sqrt(log(2 × 46 / 0.05) / (2 × 2048))`; clip the resulting interval to the endpoint's range. The bound supports uniform sampling without replacement from a fixed finite population; endpoint independence is unnecessary. See [Bardenet and Maillard, Proposition 1.2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.4029).

Individual gains and the two benchmark-relative neighborhood means have range `[-1,1]`. Their simultaneous half-width is **8.568156 percentage points**. For the neighborhood difference, the shared benchmark cancels and shared-recipe coefficients combine before bounding. Its range is `[-15/41,+15/41]`, giving a **3.134691-point** half-width. Exact rational coefficients and all bounds are stored in the plan. This preserves common-panel dependence without pretending that arms or recipes are independent.

All 44 raw win counts and rates will also be reported descriptively. They do not acquire additional confidence intervals outside this fixed family. The sampling target is the uniform finite seed population remaining after the frozen pre-draw history exclusions, under a deterministic captured engine. No inference extends to different equipment, floors, gameplay versions or future search roots.

## Practical decision and mandatory stop

The minimum practical gain is frozen at **3 percentage points**. This is a development decision threshold, not a claim that the design has high power for a true three-point improvement.

| Complete result | Required condition | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `FreshConfirmationWarranted` | At least one generated recipe's simultaneous lower gain bound is at least 0.03 | Report every qualifying recipe in party-ID order for a separate fresh confirmation design |
| `RetireBelowPracticalThreshold` | No qualifying recipe, and every generated upper bound is strictly below 0.03 | Stop this neighborhood with evidence excluding the chosen practical threshold |
| `RetireUnresolvedAtBudget` | Neither condition above | Stop this neighborhood with unresolved useful-recipe status |

Other reference teams cannot trigger discovery. A qualifying generated recipe may belong to either neighborhood; the report must retain that membership. No result promotes a proposer or qualifies a team automatically. Missing cells, altered runtime, invalid evidence or exhausted caps produce an incomplete experiment, not one of these scientific decisions.

**Precision is intentionally conservative.** At this fixed sample size, advancement requires at least **237 more wins than the benchmark out of 2,048**, an observed **11.572266-point** gain. A small real gain can remain unresolved. The sample budget is chosen prospectively as a bounded check for large improvements; it is not fitted to historical gains or a claimed optimal power calculation. The cheaper stop decision is explicit: unsupported results receive **zero additional samples**. Native implementation or admission must not silently narrow the intervals, reduce the comparison family, loosen the threshold or extend the panel to obtain a positive conclusion.
