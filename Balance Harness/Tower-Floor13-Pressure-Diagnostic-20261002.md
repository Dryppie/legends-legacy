# Floor 13: one fixed Power/penetration diagnostic

**Status: complete and independently verified; rejected for nomination, no live application.** The complete unchanged-guardian [equipment comparison](Tower-Floor13-Limited-Resistance-20261002.md) found at most **1/32** wins among limited-equipment recipes. Both original-parent baselines won **0/32**, compared with **6/32 and 7/32** using party-wide Resistance + Health equipment. The complete 192-report review attributes approximately 81–85% of recorded guardian damage to Magical damage and 64–72% to Drowned Constellation.

Test exactly one isolated setting: **offense factor 0.50** (12.83625 → **6.418125**) and **penetration factor 40** (1 → **40**). Retain Health **13.180078125**, defenses, regeneration, abilities, every other floor, progression and every raw recipe. This uses the existing typed-penetration cap and guardian scaling implementation; no combat-engine change is proposed.

The purpose is to narrow the initial damage difference between baseline and party-wide resistance gear while compensating with lower Power. This is a mechanics hypothesis, not a prediction of win rates. Equipment also changes Health, regeneration, block and other attributes. The saved reports contain prevention/mitigation telemetry flags, so exact prevented-damage attribution is not established.

Across the twenty saved initial character comparisons, the [initial-stat calculation](../TestResults/tower-floor13-pressure-rationale-20261002.json) narrows the baseline/full-gear magical-hit ratio from **1.37–1.75** to **1.18–1.26** at the penetration cap. At half Power, baseline hit sizes are **12–30% lower**, while full-specialization hit sizes remain within approximately **−3.2% to +2.3%** of their original values. This calculation excludes rounding, block, general reduction, buffs, healing, targeting and combat feedback.
