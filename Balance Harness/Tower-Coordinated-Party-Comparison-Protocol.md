## Primary endpoint: loss-penalized late Seal waves

Use the **first three Seal activations in event order** in each fight. Match starts/ends by activation ID and barrier application order; retain event-array order when timestamps tie. Collapse linked target applications into one wave per activation/effect/tick. A break after even one linked application at the same tick has not prevented that wave. Reject unmatched events or applications after a recorded barrier end; do not repair them by sorting or dropping observations.

For each of the three ordinal Seal opportunities, assign a late-wave score:

| Observed state | Score |
|---|---:|
| Started and broke | `max(0, distinct emitted waves - 1)` |
| Started and timed out | `4` |
| Still active when a victory ends combat | `max(0, distinct emitted waves - 1)` |
| Still active when a non-victory ends combat | `4` |
| Never started because combat ended in victory | `0` |
| Never started and combat ended in non-victory | `4` |

Sum the three scores as **L**, from 0 through 12; lower is better. Victory means the ordinary report's `Succeeded` value. Every other completed outcome, including a time-limit draw, is a non-victory. A failed execution is invalid evidence, not a non-victory to score. Reject a trace with more than five waves for one Seal or changed authored duration/interval; do not clamp an incompatible mechanic into this range.

The penalty uses the maximum four later waves of the pinned five-wave Seal. Truncating a losing trace cannot earn a lower score by deleting an unfinished or unstarted opportunity. A victory can eliminate exposure legitimately. Therefore L is explicitly a **mechanic-and-outcome composite**, not an unbiased estimate of Seal duration or a pure causal effect of Haste. The independent early-break and survival gates below constrain its interpretation.

For each shared value `i`, calculate `d_i = L_040e,i - L_candidate,i`. Freeze two requirements for the sole primary statistical comparison:

- Mean `d_i` across all 64 pairs is **at least 0.5** late waves per fight. This is a prospective minimum-effect screen, not an effect size estimated from historical results.
- Let `s` be the number of positive differences and `m` the number of nonzero differences. The exact one-sided sign tail is `p = sum(comb(m,k), k=s..m) / 2^m`; set `p=1` when `m=0`. Require **p <=0.05**. Compute the integer numerator/denominator exactly before formatting.

The sign test addresses whether a non-tied paired improvement is more frequent than a deterioration under the independent-seed panel assumption. It does not give a confidence bound on the mean improvement, prove symmetry of magnitudes or establish a win-rate gain. Report mean, median, all signed differences, positive/zero/negative counts and the exact tail. No bootstrap, extra random labels or post-hoc choice of test is needed. With one declared primary comparator and endpoint, no comparator is selected after results; additional gates are conjunctions, not alternative ways to pass.
