## Why the latest one-win lead does not justify a new selector

The [completed practical search](Tower-Practical-Incumbent-Tie-Preset-Execution.md) selected challenger `87b0e473…` with 25/32 selection wins versus the incumbent's 24/32. On the shared selection panel that difference was **six gained wins and five lost wins**. The corresponding independent confirmation contrast was **202 gains /196 losses**, or +0.60 points. The challenger did not qualify for promotion, but retaining the incumbent would have reduced observed confirmation wins by six. This observation does not validate treating a one-win lead as a tie.

The only other measured strict challenger lead in this census comes from allocation-comparison restart one. `7a249fb5…` led 25/32 to 22/32, with **nine gains /six losses**. Confirmation was 730/1,000 versus 708/1,000, or **+2.20 points**. That is also a descriptive result, not an adoption endpoint. We do not combine these two differently selected outputs into a population estimate or choose a new margin threshold from them.

The production [selection rule](../LL/tools/BalanceHarness/TowerBossStudyPolicy.cs#L87) explicitly preserves strict leaders and retains the designated incumbent only on a positive maximum tie. Its [completed prospective comparison](Tower-Practical-Incumbent-Tie-Comparison-Execution.md) supports that narrow rule for its frozen outputs. The evidence here supplies no basis for expanding that claim to near ties, increasing selection samples or changing parent selection.

## What the 24-root comparison measured

That comparison was designed to estimate the difference between two selectors on common searches. It measured the four roots where their outputs differed. For the other twenty roots, the difference between the two identical recipes is exactly zero, so their absolute win rates were not measured. This was correct for that endpoint.

Eight of those common outputs strictly beat the designated incumbent in selection: six novel challengers and the second supplied reference twice. Their superiority over the incumbent is **unknown**. An exact zero *between identical selector outputs* is not an observed zero *between that output and the incumbent*. The saved null win counts must stay null.

## Historical protocol requirements and design handoff

The following requirements were fulfilled by the completed confirmation linked above. They describe its prospective design, not authorization for another run.

Freeze the catalogue's **eight distinct recipes** as one new fixed family: all six challengers, designated incumbent `399bc776…` and reference `8287f779…`. Use one prospectively declared fresh common panel, the captured gameplay scope and complete current history. Run no search, mutation, candidate pruning, ability-order tuning or selection-panel reuse. The recipes are historical; their future evaluation must be a separate study with a separate endpoint, resource allowance and newly allocated values. Do not reopen the old comparison or consume its unused confirmation values.

For a strength endpoint, compare every challenger with both references and report all results. Preserving the existing practical criterion would require supported 10% viability, at least five observed percentage points over each reference and positive adjusted paired lower bounds. Under the existing Wilson-difference construction, the complete family contains **32 quantities: eight recipe rates plus gain/loss rates for twelve contrasts**. Reusing the current family-seven intervals six times would not account for this larger decision family. Which qualifying recipes can be recommended must be fixed before outcomes are inspected.

The [completed design](Tower-Practical-Fixed-Family-Confirmation-Plan.md) fixed **5,500 trials per recipe /44,000 fights**, exact viability/net-gain boundaries, one complete final look and an 82.82% conditional power lower bound under its explicit +8-point alternative and sampling assumptions. It specified one entropy batch, 48 transport bindings, all-qualifier reporting and a 100-minute /3-GiB envelope. The [final admission](Tower-Practical-Fixed-Family-Confirmation-Admission.md) bound the captured runtime and accepted scoped charge ledger. The [completed execution](Tower-Practical-Fixed-Family-Confirmation-Execution.md) consumed that admission and finished in about 39 minutes 52 seconds, retaining 934.32 MiB.
