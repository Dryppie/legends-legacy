# Affinity-creation edit diagnosis

24 September 2026. Target: the offline `LL/tools/BalanceHarness`.

**The tested proposer is a narrow Viper-insertion neighborhood: all 204 accepted edits add Viper. Forty-eight also remove a selected poison producer that the new modifier could strengthen. All thirteen measured occurrences of those producer-removing edits scored below the benchmark.** This motivates a specific development hypothesis: preserve existing partners of the affinities an addition could complete. It does not establish that the preservation rule improves search.

The new [reviewer](analysis/affinity-creation-edit-diagnosis.py) is implemented, tested and executed once. It joins **204 generated root/party occurrences**, including **72 measured and 132 explicitly unknown outcomes**, to the captured generation records. It adds **zero fights, entropy draws, reservations, policy changes or promotions**. The prior [recognition diagnostic](Tower-Affinity-Creation-Recognition-Execution.md) remains `CompleteDiagnosticOnly`; the v5 search pilot remains `Inconclusive`.

## What the join verifies

The reviewer authenticates 37 consumed files/manifest entries across the recognition review, its scientific archive, the frozen catalogue, the v5 generation archive and its admission. It reconstructs the original recognition summary with its retained producing reviewer, then independently verifies each proposal's metadata against its full parent and candidate recipes.

Checks cover exact root and party identity, source plan/policy hashes, complete accepted batches, nomination/beam membership, fixed physical context, legal canonical five-Essence loadouts, single changed owner, one- or two-slot replacement distance, exact additions/removals, selected target pair, minimal placement and newly activated authored routes. Root and party identity are both required. A measurement at another root never fills an unknown cell.

Every exported occurrence retains parent identity, wave, accepted position and attempt; owner and subgroup; equipment/progression context; removed/added Essences; target pair and route IDs; new/lost selected route activations; training stratum and inclusion probability; and the unchanged independent benchmark contrast or null. The separate [mechanics snapshot](../TestResults/affinity-creation-edit-diagnosis-20260924/mechanics.json) contains captured definitions for every added or removed Essence. It describes authored behavior, not measured trigger uptime or causal contributions.

The raw battle audits and complete-history scan are reused from the sealed publications. This diagnosis does not execute native code or rescan live reservation history. Historical score panels may occur in authenticated source files, but their outcomes and final decisions are not extracted into these summaries or pooled with recognition measurements.

## The actual proposal family

The three selected affinity routes describe two Essence pairs: Viper with Venomous Spiderling, and Viper with Spider Queen — Royal Venom. The first pair has two authored poison routes. In the captured definitions, Viper's unconditional self modifier increases its owner's Poison damage by **7%**. This is a damage modifier, not a seven-percentage-point victory-rate improvement. Viper also brings its own active ability; the measured team contrast includes all additions, removals and interactions.

All 176 one-slot edits insert Viper alone. The remaining 28 insert Viper and a missing producer. No accepted proposal edits owner 8, whose benchmark loadout already contains Viper and both producers. Across 252 attempts, the records contain **27 already-active-affinity rejections and 21 duplicate-recipe rejections**. Those are generation coverage facts, not combat outcomes.

The captured mechanics provide a reason to investigate those replacements. Royal Venom supplies a timed Basic Attack poison effect and a separate health-triggered sustain ability. Venomous Spiderling supplies an active Poison/Slow application and a conditional Basic Attack poison route. An edit can satisfy its chosen producer/modifier pair while removing the other producer, losing routes that inserting Viper could otherwise strengthen. The frozen policy protects the target pair, but it does not preserve all such additional partners.

Other removal costs remain plausible. Pack Howler supplies allied Basic Attacks, and Enchanted Fairy supplies damage, Corrosion and control. Their removed groups have eighteen measured occurrences each and negative means, but these observations cannot isolate their effects. Likewise, a positive mean for Thornstorm removal covers only six occurrences and two recipes; the Flame Imp row contains one measurement. These are insufficient grounds for an Essence blacklist, owner preference or fitted ranking score.

No owner group has a positive measured average. The complete owner/subgroup tables are in the [generated diagnosis](../TestResults/affinity-creation-edit-diagnosis-20260924/diagnosis.md). The two subgroup means are close, but neither that fact nor the owner table establishes equivalence or independence: members share root panels, and multiple roots reuse exact recipes.

## One proposed generator change

Implement an **opt-in, separately versioned creation-removal rule that retains existing endpoints of selected affinities that an addition would complete**. Keep the selected affinity inventory and the benchmark parent unchanged for the first test. Express the rule through authored endpoint relationships, without naming disfavored Essences or deriving scores from these outcomes.

For a proposed target pair on one owner:

```text
added = target pair endpoints missing from the parent loadout
possible = parent loadout union added
completeable routes = selected same-owner routes with both endpoints in possible
protected = existing parent endpoints belonging to those routes
legal removals = minimum-size legal removals outside protected
choose equally among eligible pairs, then equally among their legal removals
if none remain, record a declared rejection; do not silently relax protection
```

For this cohort, the rule would prevent inserting Viper by discarding an already equipped selected poison producer. It is distinct from preserving only affinity pairs already active in the parent: the edited benchmark owners initially lack Viper, so those producer/modifier pairs are incomplete before insertion. It also avoids a blanket rule that preserves every parent Essence.

The relevant implementation points already exist: `TowerProposalPolicies` defines the versioned contract; `TowerAffinityCreation.Opportunities` enumerates removals; `TowerAdaptiveRacingGenerator.Protected` currently computes preservation from the parent; and deterministic batch export records accepted/rejected proposals. Extend those points while retaining the exact legacy serialization, defaults and generation stream behavior. The opt-in export should record protected endpoints, eligible edit counts and explicit rejection reasons so a reviewer can reconstruct the restriction.

First verify determinism, family/copy legality, legacy equivalence, target-pair eligibility and honest handling of exhausted neighborhoods using combat-free fixtures and exports. A narrower space may increase duplicates or fail to fill seventeen unique slots; that must be visible before any admission. Existing records cannot establish the new policy's fill rate or performance because changed eligibility changes later random choices and accepted attempts.

Then freeze a small fresh, matched-budget proposer comparison with generation as the single changed factor. Keep racing and final selection fixed, retain all references and evaluate final outputs independently against the same benchmark. A filter that improves average candidate quality can still reduce useful diversity or search-output quality. Neither the thirteen negative observations nor a replay that drops them would validate the new generator. **This step has not implemented or run that policy change.**

## Limits and unchanged decisions

All grouping and the proposed rule are post-hoc development work. The groups were not new prospective qualification endpoints. No group confidence intervals or causal Essence-effect estimates are reported. All original individual family-540 combat intervals remain attached to their exact measured cells; none had a positive lower bound. The 132 unmeasured cells remain null, even where the same recipe was observed at another root.

The evidence supports a concrete mechanism to test and a narrower interpretation of the tested proposer. It does not show that preserving these routes improves win probability, that the current benchmark is optimal, or that all affinity-based, broader or adaptive search methods fail.
