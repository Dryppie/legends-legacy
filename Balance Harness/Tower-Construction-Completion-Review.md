# Fresh-construction completion: implementation and bounded comparison

The opt-in **`independent-completion-v7`** policy completed its frozen comparison with unchanged v6. Search reliability is **Fail: 0/3 passing restarts, with 2 required**. Of 12 generated finalists, **0 recorded a held-out win**. The six saved controls measured **19.92–53.12%**. The ordinary and joint-adjusted tested-family assessments are **Fail** and **Fail**, respectively.

This completes the single construction change and one capped comparison. It does not promote defaults, retune Kharad or establish near-optimality. The prior v6 **Fail**, its **131/256 (51.17%)** observed control breach, and the separate **479/1,000 (47.90%)** confirmation with adjusted interval **43.76–52.07%** remain sealed and visible. No historical samples or restart gates were pooled, and secondaries never replaced preselected primaries. A tested-family result here has its own scope and cannot become complete Tower-family acceptance.

## Implemented change

The [constructor diagnosis](Tower-Construction-Diagnosis-Review.md) found that the 50% insertion skip bypassed 1,052 incomplete characters despite an available compatible mechanic core; 971 remained incomplete after filling. Only 116/2,220 lacked a compatible insertion after coverage placement. It also found fully core-complete fresh parties that lost, so structural completion was an unproven combat hypothesis.

[TowerPartyCoverage.cs](../LL/tools/BalanceHarness/TowerPartyCoverage.cs) adds `FreshCompletion`, sharing the existing constructor and removing only the random skip for the new guided route. Every character in the already-shuffled order attempts the same legal-core selection. Coverage placement still runs first; compatible additions preserve its assignments, and characters with no legal option retain ordinary filling. The one-in-eight uniform route, missing/empty-feature behavior, family and inventory checks, weighted fill and final ordering remain intact. Existing `FreshCoverage` retains its original behavior.

[TowerBossGeneration.cs](../LL/tools/BalanceHarness/TowerBossGeneration.cs) recognizes exactly `["collective-joint", "completion-joint"]`. The comparator retains v6, while the new arm uses `fresh-completion` provenance and the same v6 refinement operators. [Input feature validation](../LL/tools/BalanceHarness/TowerBossPartyGenerator.cs) and the [provenance contract](../LL/tools/BalanceHarness/TowerBossDiscoveryContract.cs) recognize the new policy with unchanged defaults. No saved Essence ID/count, recipe, reference ancestry, old fitness or held-out result determines fresh construction.

The two methods use separate stable generation streams and shared combat schedules. This compares resulting search policies; it does not isolate mutation effects on identical parents. A complete authored pair/triple is a structural hypothesis, not proof of activation, correct recipients, timing or strength.
