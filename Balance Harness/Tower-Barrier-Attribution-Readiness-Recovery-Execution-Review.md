**16 September 2026 — DescriptiveAttributionComplete /Closed.** The approved replacement completed exactly **12 fresh values /40 fights**, with exact complete-report parity across the old runtime, updated runtime with observation off and updated runtime with observation on. This establishes usable descriptive telemetry on this finite panel; it does not establish a stronger team or universal observer equivalence.

The clearest practical finding is a **second-Seal deadline shortfall**. Every diagnostic fight emitted its second pulse at **3.9 seconds** after Seal application. Anchor 040e still had **232–462 barrier** remaining (mean **360**); 49f6 had **125–386** (mean **284.375**). Damage that subsequently finished those barriers includes substantial direct Fae's Corrosion and Venom Web contributions. Whole-fight damage totals conceal that timing. The next useful design question is whether a legal coordinated change can deliver enough damage before this deadline while preserving third-Seal performance. These observations alone do not identify such a recipe, prove the late attacks can be moved earlier, or authorize another comparison.
