## Conditional pressure lever for floor 12

If the complete limited-equipment diagnostic still shows a large specialization gap, investigate paired Power and penetration scaling before changing Volgrin's kit. The current rule in `AttributeRules.Mitigation` subtracts at most 40 percentage points after the rating curve: `max(0, 0.8 * rating / (rating + 165) - min(penetration, 40) / 100)` at zero Corrosion. `WorldTowerGuardianScaling` scales Power and raw typed penetration independently. This is an existing Tower data lever, with no combat-engine change required.

Using the twenty initial character attribute pairs in the closed historical mechanics review, baseline characters take **1.370–1.753 times** their fully specialized counterparts' post-Resistance damage from an otherwise identical magical hit at Volgrin's recorded raw penetration of **1.5840001**. At the existing 40-point penetration cap, that ratio narrows to **1.180–1.264**. For each character separately, retaining the old full-specialization hit size would require **48.67–51.45%** of the original Power; at those individual reference values, the corresponding baseline hit would be **13.87–27.89% smaller**. These are initial-attribute calculations, not a single selected setting or observed battle results.

This calculation excludes rounding, Corrosion, block, general damage reduction, buffs, targeting and combat feedback. Equipment also changes Health, regeneration, Tenacity and sometimes Power/Armor, so it predicts neither survival nor win rate. The later equipment diagnostic and saved-report review were completed before the separate 0.50/40 pressure test. That test improved limited equipment but overshot on healer controls; the finite refinement described above is the current experiment.
