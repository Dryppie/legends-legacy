Some individual seeds had earlier deaths with specialized gear, so this is not a universal improvement. The guardian regenerated at most seven health during these fights, against roughly 52,600 maximum health.

Across the four recipes, **81.52–84.55% of recorded guardian damage was Magical**, and **43.37–44.84% came from Rattling Sky**. The archived ability catalog targets every enemy with Rattling Sky and increases its coefficient as the guardian's three health thresholds are crossed. These observations suggest that surviving distributed magical pressure is an important constraint. They do not establish whether specializing only one or two characters is sufficient; the complete 110-variant comparison remains necessary before selecting a change.

However, Block Chance falls from **42.6 to 0**, Health Regeneration from **194.13 to 136.49**, and Tenacity from **160 to 0**; some Power and Armor values also fall slightly. Do not model this as an isolated Resistance increase or rewrite the saved equipment to remove those costs.

Typed mitigation totals and all guardian ability/type totals do reconcile, but exact prevented-damage attribution is not established.
