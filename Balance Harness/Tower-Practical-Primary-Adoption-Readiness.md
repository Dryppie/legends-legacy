## Smallest useful next step

The subsequent [fixed-team confirmation plan](Tower-Practical-Fixed-Team-Confirmation-Plan.md) now specifies **5,500 trials each /16,500 fights**, the existing strength gate, exact recipes, one-batch sampling, native-compatible six-slice execution and a **2,400-second /1-GiB** operational proposal. Its 82.82% conditional power lower bound assumes a true eight-point advantage over both anchors and a candidate rate of at least 25%; it is not a forecast. The next step is implementation and fixture verification of that controller. The requirements below record this review's original planning boundary. The practical goal is to decide whether to recommend this particular team; it does not require inventing a search variant or rerunning discovery to choose a different candidate.

Before any execution, that plan must resolve:

1. **Decision and complete family:** freeze this one candidate, both anchors, both comparisons, the existing supported-10% /five-observed-point /positive-paired-bound rule and its seven rate/discordance quantities. Keep recipe choice independent of all new outcomes. Encounter balance and method reliability remain separate conclusions.
2. **Sampling and precision:** predeclare one fresh shared panel, a justified exact sample size, sampling model, approximate coverage and power alternative. The practical minimum is 256 trials, not a guarantee of useful power. Do not assume the observed 8.0–8.4-point gains are known true effects or that 1,000 trials is automatically the right future budget. Three fixed teams cost **3 × N fights**; no discovery or selection fights are needed for that question.
3. **Execution compatibility:** identify or specify a fixed-recipe execution/audit route with exact recipe/cohort identity and complete evidence checks. The ordinary practical command performs a search; calling it again would answer a different question. This review supplies no runnable request, adapter implementation or assurance that a suitable route is already integrated.
4. **Admission and stopping:** pin content/settings/runtime, the complete then-current exclusion history, new output and fresh disjoint values, and concrete cumulative time/storage limits covering setup, admission, all fights, audit, publication and closeout. Do not reuse the diagnostic panel, its **1,048 unused exposed tail values**, or any closed allowance. No replacement candidate, optional extension, retry or automatic follow-up is included. A completed result missing the gate closes without promotion; an incomplete result cannot pass.

This is a planning recommendation, not a new experiment authorization. If that fixed-team question is not worth a separately justified budget, retain the promising candidate as an unpromoted measured alternative. Existing evidence gives no reason to prioritize another optimizer before resolving this concrete team decision.
