## Experiment and automatic retention

The [protocol](../TestResults/balance/tower-post-calibration-search-20260911/protocol.json) froze both studies before combat. Each generated **768 complete parties** from neutral templates: random and constructive joint methods, three generation seeds per method, 128 candidates per arm and eight paired discovery seeds. Sixteen generated parties per floor then received 64 independent selection trials. The existing policy selected two floor-1 finalists and one floor-5 finalist. All seven compatible saved builds entered only after those finalists froze; each finalist/control then received 1,000 unused paired confirmation seeds.

The historical audit excluded **89,191 integers/seeds**, using verified sealed ledgers and an additional scan of local metadata. Both new schedules are disjoint from that history and each other. The studies retain the same full 80-Essence pool, including Rare Essences, hypothetical ownership, level-1 untrained Essences, no styles/contributions and exact prior gear budgets. Floor 1 uses level 30 and Uncommon Standard tier-1/rank-1 gear; floor 5 uses level 40 and tier-1/rank-2 gear. This does not establish practical acquisition timing or isolate slots from level/gear progression.

Both studies ran through Tower Lab's normal **Find teams** path against a frozen content snapshot and the preceding calibration's verified executable. That executable also matched the currently compiled harness assemblies at preparation. Concurrent unbuilt application changes are outside these measurements. No reference ancestry or supplied starts entered generation. Search maximized strength without a 50% cap.

Tower Lab automatically wrote the three new finalists to the campaign's persistent catalog even though Kharad failed balance. An append-only merge made those records available in the main dashboard's `TestResults/balance/retained-tower-builds.json`. Before the subsequent calibration, fresh main-dashboard previews included **four floor-1 controls and six floor-5 controls**, with all new historical combat schedules excluded and zero generation starts. The original portable seven-build fixture remains intact; these additional records persist in the local results index. The [retention publication check](../TestResults/balance/tower-post-calibration-search-20260911/retention-publication.json) records the catalog hashes and preview costs.

## Verification and next action

The two studies completed **24,340/32,248 reserved combats**, including **four matching detailed replays**. Both archives reconstructed through `tower-boss-study-verify` with the retained producing executable and through Tower Lab's results endpoint. An independent audit checked candidate counts, reference isolation, all 10,000 confirmation outcomes, adjusted/pointwise Wilson intervals and automatic retention of every generated finalist. All **25 relevant backend tests passed** through `build/run-tests.ps1`.

The upper bound applies to every included party and the lower bound requires at least one supported viable party per cohort. These are separate per-floor adjusted families, not a single simultaneous claim about the whole Tower. The previous 34% / 24.25% retained-portfolio observations remain historical samples and are not pooled with these fresh results.

The accepted next action is a separate Kharad calibration using all **106 earlier portfolio parties plus all 16 new shortlisted parties**, including every earlier above-ceiling candidate. It uses fresh coarse, fine and 400-sample confirmation schedules; one linked setting must be frozen before confirmation. Garran stays at its current settings. See the [expanded-portfolio Kharad calibration](Kharad-Expanded-Portfolio-Calibration-Review.md) for its subsequent result.

After that calibration, the broader progression audit still needs the other intended cohorts: four Essences around entry, five around floor 5, six around floor 10 and at least seven at floor 11. Exact intermediate transitions and gear assumptions must be explicit; below-budget successes remain separate diagnostics. No bounded search proves the global strongest build or full Tower balance.

This search increment changes local saved-result data and documentation. It adds no engine behavior, dependency, configuration schema, migration or deployment. The subsequent authorized Kharad content adjustment is documented separately.
