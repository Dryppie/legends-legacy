## Verified findings

**All 80 historical replays matched their entire saved reports.** The full source's **16,896 saved fights** were independently recounted before execution; **1,280 saved outcomes** for the ten selected recipes were summarized without running them again. Only the frozen **80 replays** ran, on the first eight existing seeds. No new seeds, acceptance fights, confirmation, live edits or search were performed. Floor 4 remains unaccepted under the limited-equipment goal.

The key finding is a **health-pool and protective-barrier difference**, not evidence that natural regeneration alone caused the ceiling failure. Health/regeneration gear raises party starting health from **9,561 to 12,816**, adding **651 per character / 3,255 total**. Prepared regeneration rises only **68.90 → 88.97 per character per ordinary five-second pulse**. Full armor supplies **11,191** party health and **48.83** regeneration per character. These profiles also change block and other attributes, so the diagnostic cannot causally isolate the value of any one stat.

The six-item Restoration healer has **280.98 Restoration** on slot 2. Before combat damage it grants **2,852 barrier**: **2,265** from Blue Slime's Protective Slime (**453 on each character**) and **587** from Lumo Wisp's barrier on the healer. The same healer substitution on health/regeneration gear grants only **1,037** (825 + 212). The original damage teams have none of these opening barriers. Native `GrantBarrier` applies the source's Restoration multiplier; natural regeneration is a separate five-second process. The exact prepared stats and all opening grant events agree across the eight replays per recipe.

All below are existing results on the **same full 128-seed panel**, not new balance evidence:

| Composition role and gear | C wins | D wins |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| damage; restorer-specialization | 0/128 | 0/128 |
| damage; health-and-regeneration | 77/128 | 72/128 |
| damage; armor-and-health | 23/128 | 25/128 |
| healer; restorer-specialization | 33/128 | 34/128 |
| healer; health-and-regeneration | 11/128 | 13/128 |

Adding the healer at Restoration gear gains **33 and 34 wins**, with no original damage-team wins to lose. On health/regeneration gear, the changes are not monotonic: C has **5 shared wins, 72 damage-only wins and 6 healer-only wins**; D has **7 shared, 65 damage-only and 6 healer-only**. The healer substitution also removes four offensive Essences. Its different damage and protection interact with the gear; “more healing always helps” is not supported.

The next table is **mean totals before 20 seconds**, across all five original recipients and the **eight fixed replay seeds per row**. Ability healing includes passive and over-time healing; it is not an active-cast-only total. Barrier absorption is damage prevented, not regenerated or healed health.

| Recipe | Health damage received | Barrier absorbed | Ability healing | Natural regeneration |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| C/damage/health-and-regeneration | 8,364 | 0 | 35 | 957 |
| C/healer/restorer-specialization | 4,861 | 2,820 | 839 | 450 |
| C/healer/health-and-regeneration | 7,683 | 1,037 | 431 | 878 |
| D/damage/health-and-regeneration | 8,448 | 0 | 60 | 934 |
| D/healer/restorer-specialization | 5,162 | 2,847 | 632 | 354 |
| D/healer/health-and-regeneration | 7,774 | 1,037 | 432 | 832 |

The strong damage teams receive **8,364 / 8,448** early health damage, with only **35 / 60** ability healing and **957 / 935** effective regeneration. Their **3,255 extra starting health** is therefore an important competing explanation for survival. The Restoration healers receive **4,861 / 5,162** early health damage and absorb **2,820 / 2,847** through barriers—nearly all of their opening 2,852. Their effective natural regeneration is lower (**450 / 354**), alongside **839 / 632** ability healing. Those facts argue against selecting a blanket regeneration or healing reduction as the next test. They do not quantify a causal share of wins attributable to health versus regeneration versus composition.

Across the full saved panel, C/D health-regeneration damage teams average **1,846 / 1,876** effective natural regeneration and **1,291 / 1,405** ability healing over whole fights. Restoration healer teams average **1,223 / 1,315** regeneration and **2,850 / 3,112** ability healing. Duration and survival differ; these whole-fight totals are not recovery rates or controlled estimates. Potential and overhealed regeneration remain separately recorded in the evidence. The largest whole-fight ability-healing contributor for the damage teams in the replay subset is Spider Queen's Royal Cocoon; it is not a natural-regeneration tick.

Most first casualties in these replays come from Mirror Lance or Hall. Same-tick ordering was retained, and the collector counts the first death event once per replay. It does not assign a single “opening death time” to every first casualty: later casts can produce the same source ID. The full saved-panel median first death is 16 seconds for both key health/regeneration damage and Restoration healer profiles, but that median does not describe the number of surviving characters, shields, recovery or damage output.
