## Why combinations can be missed

| Problem | Supported finding | What remains a hypothesis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Construction | Core-anchored, finite completions and mandatory role coverage restrict fresh search; V5 changes one Essence on one character at a time. | Useful combinations may require a simultaneous loadout/count/subgroup change, or a team outside the role/core templates. We have not established which omitted combinations win. |
| Evaluation | Four reused discovery seeds rank tiny health differences in an almost entirely zero-win region. Whole-team fitness gives modules contextual credit, not isolated causal credit. | Remaining health may reward transient damage over sustainable winning behavior; adaptive seed noise may dominate small gains. Their relative importance is unmeasured. |
| Selection | The comparison's zero-win ties ignore selection health and preserve discovery rank; top-two nomination is already a narrow funnel. | Health-based selection could improve choice among weak candidates, but cannot establish viability or rescue an unproposed team. |
| Resource allocation | Twelve fixed starts plus four greedy edits provide little exploration of interacting changes. Historical deeper searches and larger screens also failed recovery. | A different allocation could help, but “more fights” alone is not supported as the remedy. |

The [V5 trajectory](Tower-Fresh-First-Trajectory-Review.md), lines 11–44, is particularly clear. Its best edit changed Venomous Spiderling to Bog Mite on one character. Mean boss health fell from **61.685% to 61.370%** on discovery, but the child was better on only **one of four** seeds. On selection it left **0.660 percentage points more** health; on confirmation, **0.7240625 points more**. Its exact parent was the baseline finalist. Both finalists won **0/32**. Nominations, origin merging and the implemented selector were correct.

The [execution review](Tower-Fresh-First-Comparison-Execution-Review.md), lines 3–18, records **280 completed/charged fights**: 128 discovery, 24 selection after one shared recipe merged, and 128 confirmation. The adjusted primary win-rate difference interval is approximately **[-22.22, +22.22] percentage points**. This neither establishes improvement nor equivalence. Choosing the parent under a hypothetical health tie-break would return an already observed 0/32 team, not validate a new policy.

Paired discovery seeds reduce comparison noise between candidates, but adaptation to the same panel can still select panel-specific winners. As an illustration, a fixed team with a true 10% independent-trial win probability has a **65.61%** chance of zero wins in four trials; this is arithmetic, not an estimate for these teams. Additional candidates sharing four seeds do not create additional independent evaluation evidence. Eight selection seeds also provide coarse feedback. Confirmation must stay unavailable to generation, active search-population retention and selection within that experiment. Confirmed recipes can enter a separately declared future supplied-team archive with their provenance intact.

Nor is the repository missing coordinated operators in general. Older policies have within-character pairs, cross-character changes, whole-character replacement, recombination, shared-core broadcast, coverage/count changes and a 128-loadout library ([generation](../LL/tools/BalanceHarness/TowerBossGeneration.cs), lines 68–75 and 340–372; [loadout composition](../LL/tools/BalanceHarness/TowerLoadoutComposition.cs), lines 12–33 and 68–114). A module inherits its source party's rank; a successful teammate can make an unhelpful module look promising, while copying it elsewhere can remove its enabling context. The behavior archive, diversity retention and lineage variants already address parts of population collapse. The [README](../LL/tools/BalanceHarness/README.md), lines 196–234, records failed reliability for lineage, loadout-retention and independently sampled feedback variants.

Most decisively, [deep challenger results](Tower-Deep-Challenger-Measurement-Review.md), lines 3–35 and 90–92, report **4,608 candidates**, **4,575 zero-win discovery candidates**, and **0/3 recovered primaries**. The best new confirmed challenger won 7/256, while the historical-order controls won 63/256 and 47/256. The [saved trajectory](Tower-Deep-Search-Trajectory-Review.md) found all ingredients and many shared four-Essence combinations, with every discovery winner screened; simple library eviction was not established as the explanation. These results argue against another operator label, larger beam or longer run as sufficient by itself. They do not prove that every coordinated search will fail.

## Independent discovery versus practical optimization

These are different objectives with different information budgets. Independent discovery tests whether generic mechanics and the legal pool suffice to recover strong combinations. Practical optimization asks for the best party available after using existing knowledge. Continually withholding usable saved compositions can be unnecessarily restrictive for the second objective. A persistent collection of exact recipes and provenance is useful even when their old performance is stale.

The [original plan](Automatic-Tower-Team-Discovery-Plan.md), lines 186–199, already distinguishes these modes. [TowerBossImprovement](../LL/tools/BalanceHarness/TowerBossImprovement.cs), lines 13–45 and 93–121, implements supplied-start provenance and exploration. **It cannot be launched unchanged here:** its retained operators and local neighborhood include order tuning (lines 149–187). The next policy needs a composition-only version; do not change legacy reconstruction or insert controls into `Independent` inputs.

Admission of a historical recipe requires two separate decisions:

- **Legal and comparable composition:** exact target floor, roster/subgroups, canonical Essence IDs, source families, occupied slots, pool/ownership, character/gear/attribute/training/style budget, identity and preparation state. Retain original artifacts and provenance, and give the canonical target recipe its own identity. The [retained importer](../LL/tools/BalanceHarness/TowerRetainedBuilds.cs), lines 41–62, supplies some of these checks, not a complete current-gameplay certification.
- **Usable score:** pin target content, settings and gameplay/runtime identity; discard historical fitness for current parent ranking. Charge a new discovery evaluation of each admitted start. [TowerBossReferences](../LL/tools/BalanceHarness/TowerBossReferences.cs), lines 81–99, already distinguishes historical observations from current content.

The controls are plausible composition leads, not proven current fixed-order winners. The ordering diagnostic measured original-order control 16/64 versus ascending 3/64, with an adjusted contrast including zero ([review](Tower-Composition-Order-Execution-Review.md), lines 7–24). V5's canonical controls measured **0/32 and 3/32**, versus the generated teams' 0/32. This uncertainty and the content mismatch prevent importing their old strength claims. If no compatible starts survive admission, report that condition and use an explicitly independent experiment; do not quietly repair a recipe or relabel the information contract.

## Serious alternatives

Costs below mean complete-party fights, including any imported starts. Every comparison must use identical content, fixed ordering, ownership assumptions, starting information and total stage budgets. Construction checks and duplicate attempts need separate bounds.

| Approach | Mechanism and reason to consider it | Main failure mode | Effort and equal-budget comparison |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Zero-win selection-health tie-break | Let the independent selection panel distinguish zero-win nominees. Repairs the specific stale-rank behavior. | Chooses a less damaging loss rather than a viable team; cannot change discovery coverage. | Low. Keep exact 16×4 discovery/top-two×8/32 confirmation comparison, same rule for both arms, up to 288 fights including controls. Supporting change only. |
| Independent population with coordinated blocks | Retain several different complete parties; replace multiple Essences or characters atomically; reserve construction outside core/role templates. | Combinatorial space remains large; destructive crossover and composition distance may not preserve useful behavior. | Medium. No archived starts in either arm. Can compare against the literal latest baseline at the same sixteen evaluated-candidate budget; do not call new generation labels independent baseline restarts. |
| **Supplied-start population with coordinated blocks — recommended** | Seed from compatible known compositions, reevaluate, retain useful alternatives, and combine/refine them alongside independent exploration. Avoids repeatedly rediscovering every ingredient relationship. | Anchoring on obsolete/local optima; returning an input team without improving it; mixing modules that only worked together. | Medium, reusing supplied-start and archive plumbing. | Fixed staged allocation of discovery effort | Spend a predetermined part of the same fight allowance on a broader survivor panel using separate seeds, then freeze finalists. | Early noisy pruning removes delayed-payoff families; fewer candidates receive proposals. Past feedback/rescreen failures reduce confidence in this alone. | Medium. For example 64×4 plus 16×16 costs the same 512 discovery fights as 64×8. The recommendation changes the practical information contract and how several intact combinations can contribute to a proposal. It is more substantial than changing the fresh/edit ratio, but it is not an invented claim that crossover or archives are new. The substantive hypothesis is that **compatible starting compositions, multiple reproductive parents and atomic complementary replacements together** outperform the existing supplied-start search under a fixed budget. A two-arm result tests that package; it does not identify the causal contribution of every ingredient.

## Smallest useful next implementation and verification

Implement one opt-in offline policy and its information-matched comparator, with no UI, gameplay, learned model or generic optimization framework:

1. **Explicit inputs:** for the first cohort, at most two admitted start recipes plus the ordinary pool, family map, fixed character templates and fixed ordinal order. Both methods receive exactly these starts. Keep imported ancestry on descendants; fresh proposals have independent ancestry. Any subsequent crossing with an imported descendant makes that child reference-derived.
2. **Small complete-party population:** retain four outcome-ranked elites plus up to four additional parties selected by farthest-first Essence-membership distance at fixed character slots. Seed this deterministically from the best party; break ties by current fitness then ID. Keep original admitted anchors available in a separate immutable two-entry list. No requirement for different loadouts within a team, and no scalar fitness for individual Essences/modules. Distance is an exploration device, not a strength metric.
3. **Bounded proposal schedule:** use the same initial two starts and six fresh recipes in both arms and reserve one quarter of subsequent proposal opportunities for independent legal construction. Recombination must take nontrivial contributions from at least two distinct retained parties; copying an entire donor is a duplicate, not a full-expedition search move. Choose alternative retained parents in a deterministic rotation, including diversity entries. Preserve subgroup identities; include both within-subgroup and across-subgroup pairs. Validate the completed child atomically, allowing temporarily incompatible intermediate assignments to disappear before the final legality check.
4. **Reachable legal exploration:** the fresh reserve must sample from the full declared family-compatible pool without requiring a known mechanic core or all five capability labels. Require only gameplay/cohort legality in this new mode. Role tags can remain descriptive. Do not add empty-slot, gear, training or order optimization to this first scope. Existing legality checks must be separated from version-specific role restrictions without weakening old policies.
5. **Evaluation and selection:** reuse complete-party fitness and a fixed equal discovery panel per root. Do not turn tiny discovery health gains into statistical claims. Apply the proposed zero-win selection-health rule prospectively and identically to both arms, preserving legacy positive-win ties. Freeze shortlist quotas and finalists before confirmation. Do not add adaptive racing to this first comparison.
6. **Bounded records:** reuse compact records, durable charging, source/content identity and owned-storage accounting. Log block targets, donor/parent IDs, imported ancestry, canonical identity, legality failures, exact duplicates and retained population. Stop at both candidate and proposal caps. Finite construction may skip invalid choices before emitting one proposal, but every emitted rejection/duplicate consumes its opportunity; exhausted opportunities receive no outcome-dependent refill or cap extension.

## Later bounded combat decision

This is a planning envelope, **not an allocation, execution request or fresh-seed approval request**. First pin a single target: current legal gameplay if the purpose is practical team finding, or captured content if the purpose is historical algorithm comparison. Do not mix them. Recheck both archived starts for that target without treating historical scores as current observations.

Use the two practical methods above, three genuinely varied construction roots, identical admitted starts and common initial batches within each pair. A root varies actual construction, not just a label. Give each root disjoint discovery, selection and confirmation schedules; within a root the methods and anchors use paired schedules.

| Stage | Frozen maximum | Fights |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Discovery, including both starts | 2 methods × 3 roots × 64 complete candidates × 8 trials | 3,072 |
| Selection | 4 nominees per method/root × 6 method/root cells × 32 new trials | 768 |
| Confirmation | 3 roots × (2 frozen finalists + 2 admitted anchors) × 256 new trials | 3,072 |
| **Total** | No combat replays, retries or optional sample extension | **6,912** |

Keep a finite **256 emitted-proposal cap per method/root** in addition to 64 evaluations. Stops/exhaustion are visible failures to complete the planned search, never permission to refill beyond the cap. Within-root exact recipe merges may save fights, retain every origin and do not fund extra proposals. Anchors are remeasured per root because confirmation schedules are disjoint. This avoids calling three construction starts on one common final panel independent outcome replications.

Nominate the best two discovery candidates and two distinct population representatives under a frozen deterministic distance rule; use ranked fallback only when necessary. Both methods use the same nomination rule over their evaluated candidates. Select one finalist per method/root using the versioned selection rule. Predeclare a futility stop: if fewer than two new-method roots select a recipe distinct from both admitted starts with at least one selection win, stop before **all** confirmation. This is a conservative engineering screen with false-negative risk, not a statistical failure proof. If it passes, confirm the complete declared family, including unfavorable finalists and both anchors in every root.

Success requires, in at least **two of three roots**, a new-method finalist that establishes at least 10% viability and has an observed gain of at least **five percentage points**, with adjusted paired lower bound above zero, against both the comparator and **each** admitted anchor. Do not choose the strongest anchor after observing confirmation. A possible fixed uncertainty allocation is .025 across all twelve rate cells and .025 across all nine paired contrasts, using the existing verified approximate Wilson/discordance method and complete planned family sizes. Missing evidence cannot pass. These are search-promotion criteria; preserve any above-50% team and report its separate balance implication rather than rejecting it as a search result.

At 256 trials and this multiplicity correction, a true five-point improvement will often remain unresolved. This budget can establish a clear gain, not reliably detect every small benefit. The outcome may be **insufficient evidence**; it must not trigger extra seeds, a renamed follow-up or changing the endpoint to health. Report win rates, paired uncertainty, health descriptively, novel recipe yield, proposal losses and total measured cost.

This compares the new practical method against existing retained-search machinery with equivalent information. It **does not establish superiority over the exact latest sixteen-team independent baseline**. That baseline has no archive access and a hard sixteen-party constructor cap; silently expanding it to 64 candidates would change its contract. A direct comparison with it would be a separate no-archive, sixteen-candidate experiment with equal fight schedules. That is lower priority for the stated practical goal, and is not bundled into this proposal.

**Abandon this package as the next improvement if the fixtures fail, no compatible starts can be admitted for the proposed practical cohort, the futility rule stops it, or confirmation does not meet the frozen promotion gate.** Returning the starting winner demonstrates preservation, not better search. An inconclusive result is no promotion under this budget, not proof of zero benefit. Keep the simpler comparator and the saved recipes; reconsider the objective/representation on new substantive evidence instead of another fresh/edit ratio or archive-size variant.
