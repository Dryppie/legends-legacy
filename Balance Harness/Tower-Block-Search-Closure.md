# Block-search proposal closed

16 September 2026. Offline BalanceHarness handoff after the user's instruction to proceed with the failed candidate's closure.

**End the supplied-block search proposal and retain `retained-composition` as the comparison reference.** The [quality gate](Tower-Supplied-Quality-Gate-Review.md) completed all 54 arms at equal budgets and failed both coordinated recovery and aggregate non-regression. Block recovered cross-character optima in 2/9 runs versus 5/9 for the comparator; total normalized regret was 1.750 versus 1.167. Those finite synthetic results support this stopping decision, not a claim that the comparator is combat-optimal.

No additional seed, ratio, archive-size or operator variation is queued. Historical recipes and evidence remain available, while current-gameplay strengths remain unmeasured.
