## Design and limitations

The policy completes missing authored coverage categories on characters carrying the reserved mechanic group before the existing coverage filler runs. Categories use seeded ordering. Providers are ranked by the number of legally reachable uncovered group owners, then stable seeded ties. Free slots, case-insensitive families and shared owned copies constrain insertions; reserved group Essences are retained. Each iteration fills at least one slot, bounding the work. The existing final owner-count drift rejection remains in effect.

Completion traces record category order, evidence, provider selection, compatible-provider counts and recipient changes. Old policies omit the optional trace, preserving their recorded serialization. The generator receives no control recipes, Essence-name rules or combat outcome data. Authored category presence does not establish targeting, uptime, reliability, support optimality or combat strength. No default is promoted and no balance conclusion follows from construction success.

Tests cover the corrected family case, copy exhaustion/alternatives, full slots, untouched nonowners, existing/multiple categories, evidence serialization, deterministic metadata ordering, policy registration, schedule compatibility, synthetic search budgets/provenance and cancellation. These tests use synthetic evaluators guarded against combat. The complete backend suite and fresh combat were outside this frozen scope.
