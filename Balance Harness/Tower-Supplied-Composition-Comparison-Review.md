## What the stronger fixture found

The previous integrated toy already contained its optimum in every initial batch. This fixture instead ran the actual scheduler at three frozen construction roots, with 64 evaluated parties and at most 256 proposals per arm. Its fixed scalar landscape assigns score 6 when a designated character contains neither of two target Essences, 5 when it contains exactly one, and 9 when it contains both. The lower intermediate score creates a deceptive valley. These are fabricated scalar observations, not combat scores or balance seeds.

The frozen gate required both a post-initial block-search improvement in at least one root and an optimal child descended from a retained, weaker, non-anchor parent while at least four better parties were already available. The first condition passed in two roots. The second had **zero witnesses** and failed. The [unaltered fixture](../TestResults/balance/tower-supplied-comparison-20260916/candidate/LL/tests/EssenceSystem.Tests/BalanceHarnessSuppliedComparisonTests.cs) and [TRX](../TestResults/balance/tower-supplied-comparison-20260916/control/synthetic-tests-1.trx) preserve the precise predicate and observations.

| Frozen root | Comparator initial → final | Block initial → final | Comparator / block proposals | Evaluated per arm |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| 17 | 6 → 9 | 6 → 9 | 82 / 68 | 64 |
| 31 | 9 → 9 | 9 → 9 | 91 / 66 | 64 |
| 47 | 6 → 6 | 6 → 9 | 91 / 71 | 64 |

This supports a narrower finding: block search can improve after initial construction on this landscape. It does **not** demonstrate the claimed benefit of retaining a weaker family, establish superiority, or estimate combat strength. Zero qualifying parent witnesses does not mean that weaker families were never retained, nor prove that the algorithm cannot use them on another landscape. The negative readiness gate closes this proposal under the [approved protocol](Tower-Supplied-Composition-Comparison-Protocol.md); it is not a universal claim that the search idea is ineffective. No automatic follow-on variant or fresh-seed request is proposed.
