# V11 diagnosis: diversity worked, competitive discovery did not

Completed **13 September 2026**, with **zero new combats, constructor calls, seeds or replays**. The report verifies **9,216 saved trial records, 594 cases and four fixed detailed replays**. Independent overlays check **585 proposals, 576 evaluated recipes, 220 control nominations, twelve finalist ancestries and 333 parent decisions**.

The new selector was active: all **167** v11 parent decisions used four distinct per-character loadouts. All six new finalists retained their nominated nominal control minimum, and all three fixed primary replays completed a break before their first death. Those facts rule out inactive diversity or generally missing nominal control as common explanations for this pilot’s failure. They do not identify a causal remedy.

The [closed pilot](Tower-Loadout-Diversity-Review.md) remains **reliability Fail 0/3**, all twelve generated finalists **0/256**, and ordinary/joint family **Fail / Fail**. The strongest saved control’s **131/256 (51.17%)** remains an observed ceiling breach. Its **41.33–60.92%** joint interval crosses 50%, so a repeatable rate above 50% is unestablished; the observation still prevents family acceptance. No confidence calculation, resampling or pooling was performed.

## Interpretation and next boundary

Supported: elite diversity was applied, ordered variants can differ, reservations were generally placed and retained, and control/recovery counts do not represent realized support. Contradicted as common explanations here: an inactive selector, missing nominal new-finalist control, or all losing primaries lacking a break before first death. Unresolved: the causal discovery gap, marginal value of different support/control, objective or budget changes, and the repeatable rate behind the observed ceiling breach.

Assess one source-grounded question: whether recovery coverage should distinguish effects that require a death from recovery available without a death event. Inspect all eligible authored routes and runtime trigger semantics, including summon/death attribution, before proposing any change; the assessment may reject the idea. This selects an assessment topic only, not a provider filter, quota, deadline, new fitness term or implementation. Keep control recipes, IDs, counts, ancestry and held-out results outside generation. No new campaign is allocated. Preserve the observed 131/256 ceiling breach; any fresh confirmation needs its own separately frozen family, schedules and limits without pooling. Do not retune Kharad, strengthen gear, promote defaults/catalogs or expand floors.
