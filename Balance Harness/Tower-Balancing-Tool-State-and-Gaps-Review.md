## Executive diagnosis

**The tool can construct, fight, preserve and independently evaluate legal complete parties. It has not demonstrated a reliable, efficient way to find improvements over its admitted strong teams. The missing ingredient is not simply a coordinated mutation operator.** Several such operators, broader construction policies, additional feedback, deeper searches and diversity schemes already exist. Some have useful mechanical evidence; none of the recent replacements established the required general improvement.

The most plausible scientific bottleneck is the connection between **proposing useful combinations and allocating trustworthy feedback to them**. Independent construction has demonstrably produced narrow or weak batches. Adaptive searches often make consequential parent/library decisions from four or eight repeatedly reused outcomes. Practical supplied-team search avoids the need to rediscover an entire strong composition, but its latest selected challenger still failed independent improvement criteria. These facts do not identify one dominant causal defect or justify another optimizer by themselves.

There is also a consequential, directly observable product gap: **the reliable practical experiment is assembled from reusable harness components plus study-specific launchers and decision adapters, rather than exposed as one consistent practical workflow.** Defaults still select historical order-sensitive search; the generic study reports balance/viability rather than the practical improvement decision; its newest incumbent mode is incorrectly described as independent in generated Markdown. This makes it too easy to run the wrong contract or misread a successful execution as a strength result.

My recommendation is one bounded integration increment: expose the **existing fixed-order, incumbent-preserving practical contract and its evidence-qualified result** through the normal workflow. Preserve its search behavior. This is a usability and experimental-control improvement, not a claim that wrapping it will discover better teams. **No new search algorithm, candidate variant or combat experiment is justified by the present evidence.** A later quality experiment must first have a materially distinct hypothesis and an evaluation budget capable of answering it.

### Resource, history and stopping boundaries

Schema-3 definitions bound candidates, proposals, stage sample sizes and total fights; stage schedules must be disjoint and absent from the **supplied** exclusion set. They do not independently prove that this set is the entire historical union. Plain study execution charges attempts in memory before each fight; the ordinary archive writes its trial record after combat. It is not the same crash-durable external attempt journal used by recent launchers. [contract:194–211,255–263][S8], [study:23–47][S19], [archive:38–46][S15].

Compact workflows add prepared execution, writer leases, bounded chunks, recorded attempts, retry reserves, time/storage limits and exact resume contracts. Generic CLI defaults include a 32-attempt retry reserve; the closed practical experiments explicitly prohibited retries. Owned-storage campaigns prohibit execution resume. The campaign timer starts when opened, so a generic resume timer is not evidence of a cumulative end-to-end experiment allowance. Specialized launchers account for binding, prior consumption, process ownership and verification separately. [bulk:5–8,32–88][S24], [CLI options:774–779][S1], [launch accounting:106–186][S25].

There are two history paths: the dashboard's scoped, depth-limited scanner and the complete native registry/reservation machinery. The dashboard explicitly asks callers to import history outside its results scope; it does not scan all ledger forms used by later experiments. The native registry inspects the full tree for three ledger names, rejects links, and supports the specialized recovery-aware launch path. Do not replace the latter with a shorter scan or assume the generic CLI performs it. [dashboard history:7–89][S26], [registry:12–19,22–100][S27], [launch refresh/recheck:57–93][S25].

Candidate-budget exhaustion, proposal exhaustion, cancellation, invalid evidence, confirmation capacity and scientific gate failure are distinct stops. No general “progress has plateaued, therefore optimal” stop is justified. In particular, the complete-family controller is fixed to **43,879 cells**; it is not a general practical search launcher, and its engineering readiness is not permission to begin that study. [complete-family constants:19–31][S28], [launch review:70–86][E6].

## Mechanical legality and representation

The fixed cohort is legitimate: ten level-40 characters, five Essences each, Standard tier-1/rank-2 equipment in the practical pilot, unascended/unevolved level-1 Essences, no styles or contributions, fixed identities and two five-character subgroups. Level 40 unlocks five slots through the production formula `clamp(level / 10 + 1, 1, 10)`. The production reference factory also rejects excess capacity and repeated source families. Gameplay's saved-loadout service separately checks ownership, locked slots, duplicate instances and creature-source uniqueness. [slot progression:5–8][S29], [factory:40–67][S30], [gameplay loadouts:304–318][S31].

`OwnedCopies=null` deliberately omits acquisition/account constraints. It does **not** bypass capacity, source-family or complete-party rules, and does not impose expedition-wide Essence uniqueness. A real account's roster/inventory is not missing input for this objective. The optional copy dictionary is a pooled copy budget, not a full per-account ownership model.

Slots 1–5 and 6–10 are assigned production party numbers. Allied ability targeting respects those numbers; summons inherit their owner's party number. Subgroups still share the encounter, enemy pressure, deaths and timing, so they are not independent combat experiments or additive score components. [party rules:5–20][S32], [runner:52–80][S9], [engine:3718,4817–4822,4988–4992][S33]. No mechanical legality mismatch was found for the inspected fixed-cohort path. This is narrower than proving every optional harness mode matches every live account feature.

The practical pilot used **85 Essences /82 families**. All 16 of its declared content-file hashes still match the corresponding checkout data files. That does not authenticate a build of today's dirty source or transfer old measurements to arbitrary executable changes. Historical evidence remains bound to its captured runtime/settings/identity, even when content files match.

## Evaluation and decision quality

**Fitness is a sparse outcome objective with progress tie-breaks.** Zero wins do not by themselves terminate generation. When all parties die, survival also loses discrimination and the optimizer primarily minimizes terminal guardian health. That can be useful progress information, but it does not distinguish a sustainable winning strategy from extra damage before collapse. Winning duration is averaged only over wins; it is not an unbiased general quality score. Behavior summaries affect diversity only in particular policies, not a universal alternative fitness. [fitness:181–198][S14], [behavior archive:14–29][S41].

**Selection is version-dependent.** The original generic selector uses the fitness hierarchy. The zero-win selector uses total wins, then health only when wins are zero, then frozen discovery rank. Consequently positive-win ties deliberately retain noisy discovery ordering. With multiple contexts, total wins can also disagree with the declared worst-context objective: this is a latent contract inconsistency outside the one-context incumbent pilot. The smallest remedy is to restrict this practical policy to one context or explicitly version a consistent multi-context rule, not silently reinterpret old results. [selection:50–68][S17], [incumbent constraints:250–254][S8].

**Training adaptation remains exposed to small-panel noise.** Reusing a panel is legitimate training and gives paired comparisons, but adaptive maxima are not unbiased strength estimates. In the pilot, 7/8 became 26/32 and then 167/256; its anchors moved from 4/8 to 23/32 to 161/256. This does not prove one causal explanation, but it demonstrates why small-panel rank is insufficient. The fresh-first saved trajectory is more specific: the chosen edit improved average discovery health through one favorable observation out of four, then reversed on selection and confirmation. [pilot:17–30][E15], [fresh-first trajectory:13–41][E16].

**Independent evaluation exists and matters.** The study freezes finalists before confirmation, and schedules exclude discovery/selection values. The practical pilot's adjusted paired intervals were approximately −12.07 to +16.62 and −11.06 to +15.62 percentage points. Its failure is valid under the frozen rule, but those wide intervals do not establish equivalence or rule out modest improvement. The latest subgroup candidates also fail the observed gain requirement, so the conclusion does not depend only on conservative uncertainty. Wilson-based corrections are approximate; the generic paired comparison is per contrast, while dedicated experiments allocate multiplicity over their specified families. There is no lifetime guarantee across all repeatedly designed studies. [paired statistics:8–19][S42], [pilot gate:122–130][S20], [subgroup contrasts:12–19][E17].

**Equal budgets need equal information and an explicit endpoint.** Supplied versus from-scratch modes answer different questions. Do not compare them as algorithms while giving anchors to only one. Equal combat counts also do not imply equal proposals, construction checks, novelty or wall time: the block quality gate's comparator evaluated 1,032 fresh parties versus block's 541 because rejected mutations advanced the fresh schedule. **Diagnostics are not treatment effects.** AA/BB losses end in full wipes and have earlier deaths, but outcome-conditioned damage/healing/mitigation totals are affected by survival, duration and enemy actions. ## Three highest-value directions

1. **Consolidate the existing practical workflow and result contract.** Use compatible known strong teams openly, preserve their eligibility, retain exact exports, and surface one unambiguous improvement decision separate from balance and execution. This has the strongest direct engineering justification and avoids more one-off experiment plumbing. Its limitation is explicit: unchanged search should produce unchanged teams and outcomes on equivalent inputs; integration alone cannot establish a strength gain.
2. **Design a decision-capable evaluation allocation before changing search.** Treat proposal coverage, training feedback, selection and independent measurement as a fixed total-cost system. Predeclare the minimum useful effect and compare policies with equivalent information. More training precision spends candidate coverage; broader search spends evidence per candidate; more finalists spend confirmation and multiplicity. V14 and rescreening failures constrain this direction. No allocation is recommended as effective without a new testable rationale and prospective feasibility calculation.
3. **Develop a complete complementary-party hypothesis only when new mechanics evidence supports one.** State which owners change, subgroup recipients, useful event timing, damage/support removed and the expected complete-party benefit. Alternatively, for independent discovery, demonstrate a generic construction restriction newly relevant to such combinations. This can target interactions that local rank misses, but it risks repeating failed structural/tag or mechanism-only reasoning. The present loss summaries and Ice Harpy timing suggestion do not meet this threshold.

### A later bounded quality evaluation, only after a new hypothesis qualifies

The following is the original design envelope, not allocation, launch approval, reuse of unused reservations or a recommendation to repeat a closed proposal. The [completed prospective design](Tower-Practical-Evaluation-Design.md) now supplies the comparison family, conditional power calculation and no-go decision. It distinguishes the observed usefulness threshold from the true effect assumed for power; it does not raise the former or establish search effectiveness.

* Compare **one new complete policy against the unchanged practical comparator**, both receiving the same two admitted anchors, pool, fixed cohort and canonical order. Independent-from-scratch research must be a separate contract with neither arm receiving anchors as parents. Freeze all starting information before evaluation.
* Use three prospectively declared construction restarts, with matching starting information and per-restart budgets. A concrete maximum envelope is **512 discovery fights +128 selection fights per method per restart**, ≤256 proposals, with the same incumbent eligibility and one selected output each. An allocation-changing proposal may redistribute its 640 fights only if frozen beforehand; the measured comparison is then of complete policies.
* Freeze at most six selected outputs plus the two anchors before final evaluation. At a ceiling of **1,024 new paired confirmation trials per distinct recipe**, the maximum is **3 ×2 ×640 +8 ×1,024 =12,032 fights**. Merge exact recipes with all origins retained. Fix cumulative wall/storage limits from compatible retained execution costs, including setup, history, admission, export and audit; no throughput promise follows from earlier timings.
* **Do not assume that this ceiling is statistically adequate.** Before allocating anything, perform an analytical precision/power calculation for a five-percentage-point useful gain, plausible discordance and the complete planned contrast family. Freeze the interval method and require a prospectively agreed power target, for example 80%. If this effect cannot be resolved within the practical cost ceiling, record that incompatibility and do not run an under-informative experiment or extend its sample afterward. Choosing a larger minimum useful effect is a new product decision, not a post-result adjustment.
* A prospective quality success would require supported ≥10% viability, at least a five-point observed gain and a positive multiplicity-adjusted paired lower bound versus both anchors and the same-restart comparator in at least two of three restarts, with the full planned family retained. Report each restart and all costs; do not retrospectively replace a selected primary with a better-confirmed secondary. Three restarts remain finite evidence, not a broad reliability theorem. Balance >50% remains a separate reported concern.
* Stop at the first invalidity/resource boundary or at the frozen end. Any failure to meet the complete quality gate means **no adoption**. No ratio, model, operator, seed, endpoint or sample extension follows under that proposal. Reopening requires new information or a materially different hypothesis; unresolved precision is not evidence of equivalence and is not permission to keep sampling.

The most important missing future observation is thus precise: **does a prospectively specified change improve independently measured selected output, against the same informed comparator, at equal total cost across declared restarts?** Existing logs can reveal representation and selection behavior, but cannot answer that counterfactual for an unrun method or unconfirmed runner-up.
