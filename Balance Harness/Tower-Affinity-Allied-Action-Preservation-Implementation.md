## Rule and evidence

`tower-proposal-policy-v5` and `tower-proposal-export-v5` select `preserve-completable-affinity-endpoints-and-allied-basic-attack-providers-v1`. The factory is `TowerProposalPolicies.BenchmarkAlliedActionAffinityCreation`. The rule composes the existing selected-endpoint protection with protection for each parent Essence whose equipped root ability directly contains `PerformBasicAttack` targeting exactly `NonSummonedAllies`.

`TowerAlliedActionProtection` reads the captured inventory's typed ability/effect definitions and verifies the effect node agrees with its containing ability. The report records inventory identity, provider Essence, ability/effect node keys, operation, target and effect-definition hash. Every opportunity and chosen removal records applicable source reasons, while existing coverage retains all legal edits and rejections. Missing, unresolved or inconsistent evidence rejects rather than silently dropping protection.

Names, Essence IDs, tags and historical outcome scores do not determine protection. Equipped `AbilityIds` determine roots because variants can share a base passive. Conditional effects still qualify: preservation does not claim guaranteed activation, uptime or positive value. This version covers direct ability effects; it does not infer allied attacks through statuses or summons. It protects each equipped provider, even when other providers remain.

All other proposal parameters remain unchanged: benchmark parent, selected poison affinities, 9+8 schedule, at most 128 attempts per wave, uniform eligible-pair then eligible-edit sampling, and existing family/copy/slot legality. Every current racing contract rejects the new policy before evaluator dispatch. Old optional fields remain omitted and all twelve saved v4 arms reconstruct exactly, including their metadata.
