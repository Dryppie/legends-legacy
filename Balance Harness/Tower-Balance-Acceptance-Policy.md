## Required behavior

Evaluate each floor separately at its declared progression budget, including complete party size, character levels, equipment, Essence count/training, ownership assumptions and other combat settings. The current checkpoints are four equipped Essences per character at floor 1, five around floor 5, six around floor 10 and at least seven at floor 11. Exact intervening floors and gear assumptions remain part of the [progression audit](Boss-Specific-Essence-Loadout-Plan.md#next-priority-validate-the-progression-curve).

For each floor and intended budget:

- **Upper bound: no evaluated legal party may exceed a 50% win rate.** A stronger generalist or specialist above this ceiling is a balance concern, even if the authored reference or another candidate falls inside the band.
- **Lower bound: at least one evaluated legal party must achieve a 10% win rate.** This is an existence requirement. Every weak, unsuitable or random build does not have to achieve 10%.
- The bounds are inclusive: the desired strongest viable performance is between 10% and 50%. Assess each complete party's results, never a pooled average across builds, floors or incompatible budgets. Losses and draws both count as non-wins; invalid and missing trials prevent acceptance.
- Preserve known strong recipes as controls. Continue searching for strong builds without a 50% cap on candidate selection. Do not discard high-clear builds, choose deliberately weaker finalists, lower the tested slot count or change seeds to obtain an apparent pass.

The ceiling applies to the floor's intended progression cohort. Higher-level or better-equipped transfer parties are separate evidence; their success on an earlier floor does not automatically fail that floor's intended-budget assessment. Lower-budget successes must still be reported as potential violations of the intended unlock progression. In particular, meeting the win-rate band at fewer than seven slots does not establish the intended floor-11 requirement.

## Evidence and acceptance

The target concerns repeatable performance. A small observed fraction is insufficient to establish the underlying win rate, and a bounded search cannot prove that no unsearched legal build exceeds 50%.

The evaluator keeps the observed-policy finding and sampling uncertainty visible:

| Observation in the declared confirmation set | Required interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Any build above 50%, including 10/10, 20/20 or 40/40 | Reject balance acceptance for this evidence. Report an upper-bound breach; uncertainty may qualify the estimated repeatable rate but must never turn it into a pass. |
| Every build below 10% | No qualifying viable build has been demonstrated. Do not accept the floor or infer that every possible build is unviable. |
| All observed rates at most 50%, with at least one at least 10%, but uncertainty overlaps a required boundary | Inconclusive; the observed counts alone do not establish acceptance. In particular, 1/10 and 5/10 are not automatic passes. |
| Complete frozen confirmation evidence supports an upper bound of at most 50% for every included build and a lower bound of at least 10% for at least one | Eligible for a scoped pass, subject to the declared sample, coverage and progression checks. This is not a global optimum or exhaustive balance certificate. |
| Missing required floor/build/context, invalid trial, incomplete run or mismatched budget/content | Invalid or incomplete evidence; cannot pass. |

Before running confirmation, declare exact recipes, required floor/budget/context coverage, minimum samples, combat cap, independent unused seeds and the interval/multiple-comparison method. Existing pointwise Wilson intervals remain useful descriptive evidence; they do not by themselves establish simultaneous coverage across a searched family. Do not keep sampling or replacing candidates until the result enters the band. Any later experiment needs a separately frozen protocol and its own fresh evidence.

An overall progression pass requires every required floor to pass. A strong floor must not compensate for an unviable floor, and a weak recipe must not hide an overpowered one. Keep progression assumptions fixed when comparing tuning alternatives and keep slot-isolation experiments separate from normal level/gear progression.

The implemented policy is `bonferroni-wilson-95-v1`: pointwise 95% intervals remain visible, while acceptance uses approximate Wilson intervals with 0.05 divided across the complete predeclared recipe/context family. Missing cells do not reduce that family size. Every declared floor/budget/equipment-context cohort needs its own viable party and must satisfy the ceiling for all included parties. The overall result applies only to the declared cohorts; the plan must include every required progression floor. Keep separately labeled diagnostic-budget experiments outside the intended-progression acceptance family.

The adapter verifies saved normal-Tower recipes, preparation, content/settings/execution identity, files and exact trial schedules. It does not establish that a human-authored definition predates already observed results, or that manually supplied exclusions cover every historical campaign. Freeze the protocol and unused-seed ledger before confirmation. Evaluating an old archive remains retrospective and does not create fresh evidence.

### Planned staged sampling and compact evidence

The heading is retained for existing links. The separately versioned **`tower-staged-bonferroni-wilson-95-v1`** policy is now implemented by `TowerStagedBalance` and `TowerStagedBalanceRun`, with `tower-staged-balance` and `tower-staged-balance-verify` CLI commands. It leaves the ordinary fixed-sample evaluator unchanged. [The staged confirmation review](Tower-Staged-Confirmation-Review.md) records the implementation, frozen Kharad experiment and verification.

The complete family, cohort budgets, seed-free recipes, forced anchors, two fresh disjoint schedules, second-stage capacity and battle reservation are fixed before confirmation. Historical scores may choose anchors; they never become current confirmation observations. Anchors bypass the short first look and require the larger fresh second sample. Every other cell receives the fixed first sample; an adjusted upper bound at most 50% resolves that cell. All unresolved cells advance with all anchors. The runner freezes the complete selection before any second-stage attempt and verifies that selection again on resume.

Stage one receives alpha .025 divided across all nonanchor cells. Stage two receives a separate alpha .025 divided across the complete selected family, conditional on first-stage data and independent new seeds. Its bounds use only the second sample. Acceptance combines resolved first-stage and completed second-stage bounds: all must support at most 50%, and every cohort must have at least one lower bound of at least 10%. This is approximate Wilson coverage over the frozen two-stage experiment, not exact coverage or a lifetime repeated-study guarantee.

Any completed-stage observed rate above 50% rejects acceptance. A first-stage breach stops before second-stage fights. If anchors plus unresolved cells exceed frozen capacity, the result is Inconclusive with every pending cell visible. Missing, duplicate, extra, mismatched or incomplete evidence cannot pass. No optional sample extension, pooling or outcome-based family reduction is allowed. The reservation is `(family - anchors) × first sample + maximum second-stage cells × second sample`, plus charged retry attempts. The new contract permits up to 10,000 cells and 500,000 reserved fights without increasing legacy limits. Time/storage stops retain evidence; resumption requires identical inputs and limits, and does not authorize an unbounded series of timed invocations.

The expanded historical confirmation spent 472,500 battles on 1,890 recipes that ultimately won zero of 250. This motivated the separately implemented allocation policy above; it does not justify removing those recipes retrospectively or claiming they were known to be weak before the run.

- Predeclare the complete recipe/context family, stage sample budgets, seed allocation, uncertainty method and stopping rules before any stage outcomes. Every recipe must remain covered by valid evidence, including early-resolved recipes. Preserve all observed above-ceiling findings and each cohort's supported 10% viability requirement.
- Allocate uncertainty across the entire family and all planned statistical looks. Ordinary fixed-sample Wilson intervals do not automatically support repeated outcome-dependent stopping. Keep the existing evaluator and historical fixed schedules unchanged until a new policy is implemented and independently verified.
- Predeclare any early rejection or resource stop. An interrupted run or exhausted cap cannot become a Pass by shrinking the family or accepting incomplete evidence. Later follow-ups require their own frozen protocol and fresh evidence, with an explicit account of any combined confidence claim.
- Compact evidence must preserve recipe/order, content/settings/execution identity, exact seeds and outcomes, required search-fitness fields, completeness and integrity. Deterministic full-detail replay and independent verification must remain available. Performance receipts cannot replace statistical evidence or silently trust mutable files.
- Keep execution/storage optimization separate from statistical-policy changes. Benchmark and parity checks establish implementation behavior; search-quality checks establish candidate strength. Neither substitutes for the other or for balance acceptance.

The [compact format](Tower-Compact-Archive-Review.md) and [prepared execution mode](Tower-Prepared-Execution-Review.md) satisfy the non-event report, schedule, ranking and deterministic replay checks for their opt-in evaluator path. Prepared execution changes resource use, not sampling or the statistical definition. A multi-case source requires `compactCaseId`; one verified snapshot is shared within an evaluation and never cached across evaluations. The [bulk verifier](Tower-Resumable-Bulk-Integration-Review.md) retains one chunk of full reports and compact outcome/index metadata. `tower-balance-run` campaigns execute every predeclared cell and use the unchanged ordinary evaluator; the new staged command uses the separate policy above. Retry attempts are charged separately and never enlarge the statistical sample. Each fresh calibration setting requires a compatible new definition and campaign. The all-report compatibility reader remains available for explicitly bounded callers. Diagnostic repeats do not become new balance evidence.
