- **Lower bound: at least one evaluated legal party must achieve a 10% win rate.** This is an existence requirement. Every weak, unsuitable or random build does not have to achieve 10%.
- The bounds are inclusive: the desired strongest viable performance is between 10% and 50%. Assess each complete party's results, never a pooled average across builds, floors or incompatible budgets. Losses and draws both count as non-wins; invalid and missing trials prevent acceptance.

The ceiling applies to the floor's intended progression cohort. Higher-level or better-equipped transfer parties are separate evidence; their success on an earlier floor does not automatically fail that floor's intended-budget assessment. Lower-budget successes must still be reported as potential violations of the intended unlock progression. Stage two receives a separate alpha .025 divided across the complete selected family, conditional on first-stage data and independent new seeds. Its bounds use only the second sample. Acceptance combines resolved first-stage and completed second-stage bounds: all must support at most 50%, and every cohort must have at least one lower bound of at least 10%. This is approximate Wilson coverage over the frozen two-stage experiment, not exact coverage or a lifetime repeated-study guarantee.

Any completed-stage observed rate above 50% rejects acceptance. A first-stage breach stops before second-stage fights. If anchors plus unresolved cells exceed frozen capacity, the result is Inconclusive with every pending cell visible. Missing, duplicate, extra, mismatched or incomplete evidence cannot pass. No optional sample extension, pooling or outcome-based family reduction is allowed. The reservation is `(family - anchors) × first sample + maximum second-stage cells × second sample`, plus charged retry attempts. Staged schema 1 permits up to 10,000 cells; opt-in schema 2 permits 20,000. Both retain 500,000 reserved fights and the same two-stage confidence calculations. Ordinary limits remain unchanged. Time/storage stops retain evidence; resumption requires identical inputs and limits, and does not authorize an unbounded series of timed invocations.

The expanded historical confirmation spent 472,500 battles on 1,890 recipes that ultimately won zero of 250. This motivated the separately implemented allocation policy above; it does not justify removing those recipes retrospectively or claiming they were known to be weak before the run.

- Predeclare the complete recipe/context family, stage sample budgets, seed allocation, uncertainty method and stopping rules before any stage outcomes. Every recipe must remain covered by valid evidence, including early-resolved recipes. Preserve all observed above-ceiling findings and each cohort's supported 10% viability requirement.
- Allocate uncertainty across the entire family and all planned statistical looks. Ordinary fixed-sample Wilson intervals do not automatically support repeated outcome-dependent stopping. Keep the existing evaluator and historical fixed schedules unchanged until a new policy is implemented and independently verified.
- Predeclare any early rejection or resource stop. An interrupted run or exhausted cap cannot become a Pass by shrinking the family or accepting incomplete evidence. Later follow-ups require their own frozen protocol and fresh evidence, with an explicit account of any combined confidence claim.
- Compact evidence must preserve recipe/order, content/settings/execution identity, exact seeds and outcomes, required search-fitness fields, completeness and integrity. Deterministic full-detail replay and independent verification must remain available. Performance receipts cannot replace statistical evidence or silently trust mutable files.
- Keep execution/storage optimization separate from statistical-policy changes. Benchmark and parity checks establish implementation behavior; search-quality checks establish candidate strength. Neither substitutes for the other or for balance acceptance.

The [compact format](Tower-Compact-Archive-Review.md) and [prepared execution mode](Tower-Prepared-Execution-Review.md) satisfy the non-event report, schedule, ranking and deterministic replay checks for their opt-in evaluator path. Prepared execution changes resource use, not sampling or the statistical definition. Retry attempts are charged separately and never enlarge the statistical sample. Each fresh calibration setting requires a compatible new definition and campaign.
