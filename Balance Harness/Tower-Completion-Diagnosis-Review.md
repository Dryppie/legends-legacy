# Completion failure: saved-evidence diagnosis

The completed v7 pilot's failure is not explained by universally dormant mechanic cores. All **25 condition-core instances** in the three fixed new-primary replays have matching evidence for both constituent nodes. Their parties nevertheless lose their first character at **42.0–42.9 seconds** on the full saved validation schedules, versus **70.0 seconds** for the original anchor. Kharad's Seal pulse accounts for **66.98–71.40%** of logged incoming final-health-damage telemetry in those three replays.

A subsequent audit of all **80 eligible Essences** found **nine potential direct attribute-mitigation effects across six Essences**. **Five Essences are absent from both protection coverage and protection intent**, despite authored Armor, Resistance or DamageReduction routes. This is a concrete extraction gap; it does not prove that adding those features will produce winning parties. The [next attribute-defense plan](Tower-Attribute-Defense-Plan.md) isolates that hypothesis against unchanged v6.

This diagnosis ran **zero new fights, zero constructor calls and zero new seeds**. It read all **576 saved search parties / 4,608 discovery records**, all **18 validation cells / 4,608 validation records**, and exactly the **four existing detailed replays**. V7 remains **Fail, 0/3 restarts**, with all 12 finalists at **0/256**. Its **136/256 (53.125%)** control breach and tested-family Fail remain unchanged. The older **131/256** breach and separate **479/1,000** Inconclusive confirmation remain separate, with no pooling or confidence update.

[Core evidence](../TestResults/balance/tower-completion-diagnosis-20260913/core-evidence.json) maps every equipped instance to frozen authored definitions and same-owner log observations. It resolves all 48 core definitions. The 25 condition instances in the three primaries all have effect evidence; all 37 instances have at least observations or the explicitly weaker cast proxies. The original anchor has only five structural instances, all observed, and still lasts longer in this fixed replay. Core count cannot rank these parties' competitive strength.

There are important limits to this evidence. `PerformBasicAttack` calls the recipient's basic attack without logging the originating effect ID. A Coordinated Attack cast is therefore only a proxy: the log cannot distinguish its forced attacks from natural attacks or attribute resulting listener effects to that producer. Standard-condition application logs preserve an ability name and condition, not the exact effect ID. The audit labels those observations accordingly. Same-owner co-observation does not prove the proposed producer caused the consumer; another party member may supply the condition. Missing log evidence would likewise not prove inactivity.

Incoming telemetry was reconciled against all ten initial friendly statistics. `finalHealthDamage` can include overkill, so these shares describe logged damage allocation, not capped health lost or deaths a defense would prevent. The survival comparison does not establish whether more mitigation, healing, damage, control or faster barrier breaking is the best causal intervention. It does justify inspecting whether the feature inventory recognizes available defenses.

## Defensive feature gap

[The static audit](../TestResults/balance/tower-completion-diagnosis-20260913/defense-audit.json) scans direct authored abilities for all 80 eligible Essences. It records complete effect and trigger definitions for nine potential positive Armor/Resistance/DamageReduction routes. The six associated Essences include one existing protection provider; five are missing from both the coverage list and intent signal. Their IDs are diagnostic inventory output only; the proposed classifier must use general operation, attribute, recipient and value rules.

| Missing provider | Authored potential route |
| --- | --- |
| Bark Golem | Armor as a percentage of initial value |
| Giant Spider | Armor modification |
| Hollow Stag | Four conditional DamageReduction modifications |
| Horned Wolf | Armor synchronized per living nonsummoned ally |
| Lumo Sentinel | Resistance modification |

The current [coverage extractor](../LL/tools/BalanceHarness/TowerPartyCoverage.cs) recognizes barriers, cover, selected conditions and negative damage-taken modifiers. It does not include these attribute routes. The [inventory intent extractor](../LL/tools/BalanceHarness/TowerBossInventory.cs) has the same omission. They remain available through ordinary and uniform sampling; they are not banned from legal parties. The static audit is limited to direct authored effects with a positive base or coefficient, and does not claim complete nested-route coverage, guaranteed positive runtime magnitude or uptime. Those semantics are explicit prerequisites in the next plan.

The existing protection category also mixes damage-type-specific reductions: Poison and Burn protection appear alongside general barriers, while the dominant recorded pulse is Magical. That suggests a future typing question, but the proposed next experiment adds only the missing attribute routes. It does not change damage-type weights, remove existing providers or use saved performance to assign counts.
