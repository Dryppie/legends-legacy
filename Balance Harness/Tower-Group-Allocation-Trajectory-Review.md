## Interpretation and next decision

Neither policy assembled the full control-shared four-Essence combination in any of its 44 evaluated teams. All 88 teams had zero discovery wins; all four screens had zero wins and retained discovery rank 1. This locates the observed gap before confirmation; it does not prove that copying the shared ingredients would recover the controls.

The allocation variant changed how remaining slots were assigned, while reducing the number of different groups reached by this small budget. Its confirmation finalist still left 80.44% boss health versus 79.87% for completion; both won 0/32. The controls left 27.40% and 31.50%, winning 0/32 and 2/32. Those health differences are descriptive, and the primary adjusted win-rate interval remains −22.22 to +22.22 points. No general search improvement is established.

Do not promote allocation or fund a larger fight batch from this result. The next engineering question is whether generic construction can assemble compatible multi-role loadouts at all, with coverage and targeting derived from authored mechanics rather than hardcoded control Essence IDs. Evaluate that question with zero-combat construction fixtures and an explicit coverage measure before proposing another combat comparison. This diagnosis provides no authority for fresh seeds or fights.
