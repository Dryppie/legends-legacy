## What the trajectory shows

The unchanged search evaluated 64 parties from 73 proposals: 64 evaluated, seven duplicates and two duplicate-family proposals. Discovery used eight shared values per party. The four nominees scored **8/8, 6/8, 0/8 and 0/8** there, then **7/32, 9/32, 0/32 and 0/32** on independent selection. The 9/32 nominee became the finalist and subsequently won 66/256. Positive selection wins decided this choice; the zero-win health tie-break did not determine the finalist.

Both supplied anchors were evaluated during discovery: 040e won 6/8 and ranked 3; 49f6 won 4/8 and ranked 13. Neither reached selection. The frozen shortlist rule kept the best two discovery ranks plus two diversity representatives, including the two zero-win teams. A lower-health 6/8 discovery candidate outranked the stronger confirmed anchor. Native reconstruction verified that this was the declared policy, not a dispatch error. The [saved trajectory summary](../TestResults/balance/tower-retained-practical-pilot-execution-20260916/trajectory-summary.json) records all six relevant candidates and their ranks.

This run gives concrete evidence that discovery ranking and nomination can discard valuable supplied knowledge. Adaptive reuse of only eight discovery values is a plausible contributor; this one root cannot separate sampling noise, adaptation and construction effects. No anchor selection results exist, so we cannot claim what a revised selector would have chosen. We also cannot infer the confirmation strength of every discarded candidate.

**Recommended next design step:** retain admitted anchors as eligible incumbents through independent selection, with a prospectively fixed allocation of challenger and incumbent slots under an explicit equal fight budget. Define the practical output as the best independently supported incumbent or challenger. This addresses the observed regression path before another mutation variant. Any later implementation or comparison needs its own bounded scope; this pilot authorizes none. Preserve this failed outcome and do not retrospectively relabel an anchor as its selected finalist.
