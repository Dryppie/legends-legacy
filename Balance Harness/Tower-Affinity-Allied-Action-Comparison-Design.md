## Frozen outputs and decisions

The selector freezes one non-benchmark challenger using the 16-value nomination panel. It then compares that challenger with the fixed benchmark on a complete 60-value paired panel. Its unchanged exact gate requires more gained than lost wins and a one-sided discordant-pair binomial tail of at most 1/20; failure returns the benchmark. This is a provisional output gate, not independent team confirmation or adoption.

All 24 complete search outputs must freeze before the first held-out observation. Within each root, evaluate the control output, candidate output and fixed benchmark on the same 256 held-out values, deduplicating identical physical recipes. Identical outputs have an exact zero method contrast; keep their absolute benchmark results. Shared physical search observations must agree across arms, while search requests remain separately charged.

The primary endpoint is the equal-root mean held-out candidate-minus-control win-rate difference. The guardrail is the equal-root mean candidate-minus-fixed-benchmark difference. Preserve all twelve root contrasts, mean, median and worst result, with the existing descriptive 95% t interval using 11 degrees of freedom. Report paired-seed standard errors and covariance separately, conditional on the frozen outputs. Repeated recipes and paired fights must not be treated as independent root replications.

The preceding native plan's analysis object is retained exactly. Apply its rules in order:

1. Any incomplete work, failed audit or failed publication makes the study invalid.
2. Abandon this configuration if either mean contrast is at most −2 percentage points.
3. If no root has a different output, report `NoObservedOutputDifferentiation`.
4. A larger fresh evaluation is warranted only if the method mean is at least +2 points, the benchmark mean is nonnegative, and at least three roots differ.
5. Otherwise report `Inconclusive`. No result adopts the policy.

Retain both arms' all-root gate counts, gains/losses, exact tails, fallbacks, selected parties, novelty, generation attempts, rejection reasons, unique recipes and protection evidence. Removal counts by Essence and owner remain descriptive. The study cannot rescue a failed final-output comparison by choosing a better ungated nominee, a retrospectively preferred recipe or an improved subgroup. Generated-pool quality would require a separate diagnostic design.
