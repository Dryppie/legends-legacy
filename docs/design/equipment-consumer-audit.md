## Leaderboard transition contract

[LeaderboardService](../../LL/src/Infrastructure/Service/Services.LL/Leaderboards/LeaderboardService.cs) resolves the exact saved Soul Archive definition for each distinct version found among eligible participants. It does not infer cohorts from version ordering or the currently published quest list. Characters without saved progress follow the existing quest-integration fallback. Turning publication flags off does not reclassify saved Forge characters.

[LeaderboardRepository](../../LL/src/Infrastructure/Persistence/Persistence.LL/Repositories/Leaderboards/LeaderboardRepository.cs) applies legacy eligibility before scoring, ranking, paging, search and viewer lookup. Total Level still means combat plus profession levels, restricted to legacy characters. Combat Level retains combat level/XP scoring across both cohorts. Other boards retain their existing rules. Existing keys/cursor formats remain valid; an anchor no longer eligible resets to the first page. These are live standings, not archived rank snapshots; stored profession levels/XP and quest history are not rewritten.

The Tavern waits for the access endpoint's saved-cohort result before loading standings. Forge characters start on Combat Level without Total Level or Professions navigation. Legacy characters retain their default and choices. Failed or missing cohort access shows a retry. Entering the page clears cached standings and invalidates responses from a previous visit/character.

## Guide and tutorial transition contract

[HelpService](../../LL/src/Presentation/ll/src/app/shared/help/help.service.ts) resolves affected guides through the existing equipment access endpoint's saved-cohort field, rather than inspecting a possibly unloaded journal or action flags. The [guide catalog](../../LL/src/Presentation/ll/src/app/shared/help/guide-catalog.ts) maps seven affected IDs. Crafting/equipment/tool introductions use the Forge guide; Inventory, Essence-loadout and Lumo use their own modern variants. The legacy `tutorial-crafting` alias resolves to the existing Crafting guide.

The drawer shows loading while the cohort is unresolved and its existing close/reopen recovery message on failure or missing cohort data. Static assets are cached by URL; cohort decisions are rechecked on each open/retry. Destroying the drawer releases its cohort subscription. Unaffected guides remain independent of equipment access.

[QuestPresenterService](../../LL/src/Presentation/ll/src/app/core/services/api/quest/quest-presenter.service.ts) selects the modern Lumo tour from the active quest's saved `requiresEquipmentProgression` flag. The tour targets the existing Lumo card and battle control, without the hidden gathering panel. It teaches the saved modern quest's victory requirement. Deferred starts recheck the objective/route before launching. Stored quest definitions and legacy tour files remain intact.

The title/Soulstone review found existing retirement handling in [AchievementService](../../LL/src/Infrastructure/Service/Services.LL/Achievements/AchievementService.cs) and [SoulstoneBonusProvider](../../LL/src/Infrastructure/Service/Services.LL/Bonuses/SoulstoneBonusProvider.cs); no new backend behavior was required. This does not certify every independent title grant, generic tooltip or bonus provider.

## Shared tooltips, bonuses and activity gates — 3 September 2026

The [dungeon hub factory](../../LL/src/Core/Application/UseCases/Dungeons/Queries/GetAvailableDungeons/GetAvailableDungeonsQuery.cs) uses its existing saved-cohort policy to set an additive GatheringEnabled capability. Modern mastery summaries report zero gathering bonus and omit definitions explicitly marked RequiresGathering. The player card hides its gathering tab and summary, resets an open retired tab on refresh, and continues to show visibility, rest, combat-Vigor and completion-currency benefits. Levels 3 and 8 remain earned mastery levels; this change does not invent replacement rewards or rewrite history. Legacy previews retain all ten benefit entries.

The [shared item description helper](../../LL/src/Presentation/ll/src/app/shared/utils/inventory/item-description.ts) now reads the Soul Archive quest's saved flag for base/instance popovers. It does not infer a cohort from some unrelated quest, version order or an empty journal. A missing flag suppresses only progression-specific instructions until a usable journal arrives. Historical legacy equipment remains inspectable; canonical [equipment displays](../../LL/src/Presentation/ll/src/app/shared/components/equipment/equipment-display.ts) ignore any leftover crafting-roll/design metadata and do not present production budgets as canonical comparisons.

Bonus review covered both IBonusProvider registrations: Soulstone retirement already filters retired upgrade IDs, and [Essence Codex validation](../../LL/src/Infrastructure/Service/Services.LL/Essences/JsonEssenceCodexCollectionDefinitionProvider.cs) accepts only EssenceExperienceGainBps, EssencePityProgressionGainBps, FocusedMonsterEssenceDropRateRelativeBps and EssenceDropRateRelativeBps. All 19 authored collections use those kinds. Guild profession orders/building text already have cohort-specific paths. Tool-affix and old Tempering tooltips remain for real legacy holdings and historical results; these do not grant modern gathering rewards or re-enable equip/start requests.

Gate review covered [combat areas](../../LL/src/Infrastructure/Service/Services.LL/Quests/CombatAreaAccessService.cs), [dungeons/sigil assembly](../../LL/src/Infrastructure/Service/Services.LL/Dungeons/DungeonAccessPolicy.cs), [region bosses](../../LL/src/Infrastructure/Service/Services.LL/RegionBosses/RegionBossService.cs), [Tower participation](../../LL/src/Infrastructure/Service/Services.LL/WorldTower/WorldTowerService.cs) and [Colosseum](../../LL/src/Infrastructure/Service/Services.LL/Colosseum/ColosseumService.cs). Their direct requirements concern combat/quest progression, sigils/clears, participation state or Power Rating. The retired `quest.onboarding.tools_of_trade` gate has been replaced by `quest.onboarding.first_weapon`, so Lumo opens after the player equips the one-handed weapon selected from the Arms Chest. This review does not establish later-tier acquisition coverage or replace the pending legacy Power Rating calibration migration. Raids remain deferred.

Verification: **61 focused backend tests** passed through a fresh build/run-tests.ps1 run, including four new hub/cohort cases and existing dungeon access, mastery, reward and retirement coverage. **48 browser tests** passed for shared popovers, equipment displays and dungeon cards; the player development build passed. The first backend run exposed a test fixture with no gathering loot; the fixture was corrected and all cases passed. The final backend build had two existing xUnit analyzer warnings and no errors. Full-suite, authenticated gameplay and PostgreSQL acceptance were not run. No migration, activation, configuration change, conversion, LiveOps edit or deployment occurred.

## Operator inspection contract

The [equipment snapshot loader](../../LL/src/API/API.LiveOps/Support/LiveOpsPlayerSupportSnapshotService.Equipment.cs) inspects only equipment linked to the target character through inventory, equipped slots, marketplace escrow or a current guild loan. It combines location labels and counts each item instance once. It does not attribute all guild property or previously transferred property to the character.

The snapshot displays frozen identity, archetype, tier, rank, balance version, native/active style, binding/owner, original award source and ID, base salvage basis, and paid rank investment entries with operation IDs. Paid totals describe recorded rank payments, not all lifetime Forge spending or a current salvage quote. Legacy gear stays explicitly legacy; granted ranks have no fabricated paid basis.

Unclaimed protected reward receipts retain their run/item identities and secured timestamps. Current target counters are shown separately, so an old pending claim is not confused with a newer target. Ordinary progress uses its stored commitment thresholds and fractional Scrap remainder. Learned styles retain free-application usage IDs. Reading the snapshot never settles combat, claims items, grants resources, changes targets or refreshes equipment stats.

Lists are capped at 100 rows each and ordered by stable identifiers, not represented as chronological history. Counts/truncation distinguish a sample from complete holdings. The section has its own context and existing timeout/error handling, so missing schema or a failed read does not discard other support sections.

The [dungeon equipment loader](../../LL/src/API/API.LiveOps/Support/LiveOpsPlayerSupportSnapshotService.DungeonEquipment.cs) inspects the one retained run for the character, regardless of status. It projects identity/status/timestamps and the frozen commitment without loading room or combat-snapshot graphs. Saved reward rows are counted independently and limited to 100 by identifier. The protected receipt lookup uses both character and run ID and includes claimed receipts for the retained run. Unclaimed receipts remain independently visible if their run has been removed.

Entry terms show the saved pool/dungeon, difficulty, matching chance, guarantee threshold, completion Scrap and optional target identity/rank/style/balance version. A target is a possibility, not an award; the recorded outcome determines final binding/salvage. Current protection targets and current content do not replace the frozen descriptor. A missing commitment differs from a commitment with no selected target. Run and receipt claim timestamps are displayed separately.

Saved run rows and the receipt can describe the same item and Scrap. They are not summed, added to owned equipment counts or represented as a complete loot/currency total or claim preview. General secured/pending loot bags and currencies remain outside this equipment view. These are observational reads, not a transactionally consistent reconciliation or an entitlement calculation. The existing equipment-section timeout/failure boundary and administration read permission still apply. Older responses without dungeon inspection are distinguished from an explicit null run.

Remaining operator work: lookup/pagination for complete histories beyond the snapshot, full secured/pending loot and currency inspection, general queued holdings, conversion reconciliation/correction tools and observed progression/economy telemetry. No conversion or activation is implied by this inspection feature.
