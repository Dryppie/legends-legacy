## Decisions

- Equipment progression state is an immutable domain value carried by an optional descriptor on the existing `EquipmentInstance` and `EquipmentSnapshot`. Legacy rows retain null descriptors and their existing interpretation. Equipment progression uses its frozen stats and normalized rating units without legacy base modifiers, rarity multipliers, Quality, Potential or item XP.
- `ModelVersion` identifies this new item-state shape; `BalanceVersion` identifies immutable configuration/content. Neither renumbers the shared rarity enum nor changes legacy `StatModelVersion`. Equipment progression rarity categories explicitly retain Common=0, Rare=2, Legendary=5.
- Stat allocation reuses the existing Core budget allocator, v17 exchange rates, tier scaling and attribute quantizer. The proposed 25% tier growth is not adopted independently of normalized rating behavior. Base budget, style allocation and per-rank increment are constructor inputs to an immutable balance configuration.
- The default styled distribution is 85% archetype / 15% style within one budget. Ranks add 4% of baseline each. Rarity, native identity and provenance do not multiply the budget. Definitions freeze copied normalized weight maps so caller mutation or enumeration order cannot change evaluation.
- Styles cannot modify equipment type, handedness or weapon/armor behavior. Their mechanical outputs are stat distribution and one active set ID. The existing equip rules and shared combat/set resolvers also calculate equipped Forge previews; a two-handed item counts once even when occupying both hand slots.
- The allocator redistributes within a profile, then uses explicitly authored archetype overflow weights. An unspendable budget is rejected. Rank transitions also reject a zero or negative change after quantization, preventing a nominal upgrade with no representable item improvement.
- Awarded ranks start with no paid receipts. Rank operations retain the actual charged Scrap/Cinders and operation ID. Replaying a recorded operation leaves state unchanged; conflicting payment details fail. Salvage derives from those receipts and the award's saved base entitlement, never current prices or the displayed rank.
- Discoveries may remain unbound and retain authored base salvage. Other award types bind on personal award and default to zero base salvage, including migration/admin awards. Explicit audited migration compensation is deferred; no historical paid value is inferred.
- Personal equip, rank improvement or a style change binds equipment. A no-op style change leaves state unchanged. Native/plain restoration requires no learned Blueprint; another style requires learned compatible knowledge. Guild donation preserves guild ownership and blocks personal mutation/salvage.

Domain transitions remain pure. The Forge commands now validate prices, balances, ownership, item availability and combat state, then persist debits, item changes, learned-style entitlements, economy entries and operation receipts through the existing transaction pipeline. Calling the domain transition alone never debits or mints currency.

## Persistence and starter acquisition

- The immutable descriptor is stored as JSONB. Loading an item or snapshot does not rerun the current evaluator. A later paid upgrade replaces the live descriptor without changing older snapshots or the original starter grant. Item and snapshot columns keep legacy-compatible tier/rarity/set fields; frozen stats remain authoritative for equipment progression.
- `StarterEquipmentGrants` has primary key `(CharacterId, Kind)`. It retains original awarded descriptors independently of inventory/equipment location, with distinct item IDs for duplicate dual-wield choices. Replays return the original IDs and never issue new gear, including before the first save in a shared transaction. Changing an already claimed choice fails.
- `ClaimStarterEquipmentCommand` uses the existing command transaction, process character lock and PostgreSQL advisory lock. Inventory, acquisition ledger, loot history, realtime outbox and the grant receipt use that transaction. The service finishes prerequisite, choice and item-base checks before adding the receipt or inventory items. Repositories own all EF access.
- `FirstWeapon` requires completed `quest.onboarding.soul_archive` and accepts one Head, Chest and Legs choice plus either one two-handed weapon or two legal hand items. Armor choices are independent. Two identical one-handed choices produce two distinct instances.
- `ReadyForRoad` requires completed `quest.onboarding.first_weapon`; an empty selection grants `band`, `amulet` and `vial`. The equipment progression First Weapon completion automatically grants the accessory kit in the same quest reward transaction. Starter claims themselves grant no Cinders/Scrap; the quest reward pipeline handles those currencies.
- Both stages issue Common, Tier-1, rank-0, plain, personally bound equipment with zero salvage basis and no paid investment. Catalog IDs are `plain.<item-base-id>` in lowercase; exact item-base IDs retain their existing casing. Runtime starter content does not read production recipes.
- Instance-level binding is exposed in inventory/equipment DTOs and checked by legacy transfers/marketplace. Unbound discoveries now transfer their frozen owner through direct transfer and marketplace delivery without reevaluating stats or changing provenance/salvage. Recipient equip binds them. Legacy salvage and Tempering reject equipment progression; the dedicated guild donation/borrowing flow is now connected below.

## Forge backend

- `ImproveRank` pays the authored next-rank Scrap/Cinders exactly once, stores those actual payments on the item and binds it. Tier-1 ranks 0–5 currently use Scrap `[5, 10, 20, 40, 80]` and Cinders `[250, 500, 1000, 2000, 4000]`. These are provisional implementation inputs, not a balance pass. Rank 5, unsupported tiers, edited frozen content and insufficient funds cannot charge.
- `LearnStyle` consumes one matching Blueprint book and unlocks that style across every compatible archetype for the character. Learning a known style succeeds without consuming another book. The separate learned-style row retains one free application, consumed by the first real application to any compatible item. Competing pre-free-use quotes become stale after that entitlement is spent.
- `ChangeStyle` costs 250 Cinders after the free use. Applying the active style is a free no-op. Restoring native/plain style needs no Blueprint knowledge and charges the ordinary fee for a real change; native restoration does not consume a learned free use. Rank, identity and paid rank basis survive style changes. Style fees never become salvage basis.
- `Salvage` requires personally owned, unequipped, available inventory equipment. Favorites require the explicit override. Refund is the frozen award's base Scrap plus floor(50% of actual paid rank Scrap); awarded ranks do not create paid basis. It destroys the item and credits Scrap once, returns no Cinders and rejects refund overflow. Current starter/plain definitions have zero base salvage. Batch salvage is not exposed.
- All four writes are `ICommand<Response<T>>` requests using the character command lock, PostgreSQL advisory lock and transaction. The repository also locks the character row to coordinate incoming/outgoing transfers. Equipment row versions, the learned-style entitlement concurrency token and composite operation keys protect persistence. Listed, queued, vault-held, foreign, inconsistent-location and stacked equipment are rejected.
- Every successful operation records the exact response independently of item lifetime. Retrying the same operation ID and original request/token returns that response even after salvage or a later upgrade. Reusing that ID with different input fails. A completed no-op also receives a replay receipt, but emits no Forge event or economy entry.
- Read-only previews contain before/after frozen item stats, binding, price version, item version, balance version, exact costs/refund, free-use status and an expiry. Equipped previews include total attributes, active set bonuses and set-granted abilities before/after. Quotes expire on UTC five-minute boundaries, so remaining validity may be less than five minutes. The opaque token covers the operation/request, frozen state, price, learned entitlement, location, level and equipped loadout. Execution recomputes it; stale requests return a fresh quote with no Forge charge.
- Equipped changes settle earned idle combat using old equipment before mutation. A remaining backlog blocks the Forge operation. If settlement changes the loadout/level/quote, the client receives a fresh quote. Already earned combat progress is retained and its state is invalidated even when the Forge cannot proceed. Previously captured combat snapshots are unchanged.
- Actual mutations enqueue `equipment.model_e_forge_completed`; equipped mutations also enqueue the existing `EquipmentChanged` event. Currency/item flows receive economy audit entries tied to the operation ID. Forge commands grant no legacy crafting XP, attempt achievements or Soulstones. `equipment-forge`, inventory, equipment and affected character summaries are invalidated through the existing synchronization pipeline. Quest equipment objectives inspect saved grants and currently equipped archetypes; spending at the Forge is not an onboarding requirement.

### Forge API

The versioned `Forge` controller uses the authenticated character; callers cannot supply another owner:

- `POST preview`: `{ "kind": "ImproveRank", "itemInstanceId": "..." }`. `ChangeStyle`/`LearnStyle` include `styleId`; `Salvage` may include `allowFavoriteSalvage`.
- `GET styles?itemInstanceId=...`: style knowledge, free application, compatibility and native/active status. An empty item ID lists knowledge without item compatibility.
- `POST rank`: `{ "operationId": "...", "itemInstanceId": "...", "quoteToken": "..." }`.
- `POST style`: the same fields plus nullable `styleId` (`null` restores Plain).
- `POST learn`: the same fields plus `styleId`, with the book instance as `itemInstanceId`.
- `POST salvage`: the same fields plus `allowFavoriteSalvage`.

The client echoes the preview's operation ID and token and keeps them for network retries. On a fresh-quote response it must show the changed proposal before confirming again. Mutations return `Outcome` or `FreshQuote` inside the usual response envelope.

`EquipmentProgression:ForgeEnabled` defaults to `false`; no environment settings changed. The API and worker package the style/price files through their existing content rules. The additive migration `20260902162834_PersistModelEForgeReceiptsAndLearnedStyles` creates only the learned-style and receipt tables with character foreign keys. It follows the earlier equipment/starter migration and must precede enabling Forge. It was generated locally and has not been applied to a database. Downgrading would discard learned knowledge, spent entitlements and retry receipts.

## Protected dungeon acquisition and baseline recovery

- Runtime content defines Goblin Mines and Forgotten Catacombs across difficulties I–III: six independent pools, each with eight named targets. Goblin targets use Fury/Phoenix; Catacombs targets use Arcane/Endurance. All currently award Rare Tier-1 rank-1 equipment with its native style. The existing 31 plain starter options are unchanged.
- Provisional terms are a 20% matching chance, a guarantee by the eighth qualifying completion and four Tempered Scrap per completed run. A selected target is guaranteed on the first difficulty-I clear. Guaranteed rewards are bound with zero base salvage; ordinary matching discoveries are unbound with two base Scrap and no paid rank investment. Existing first-clear Blueprint rewards remain intact.
- Source previews expose target stats, selected identity, progress, eligibility and first-clear guarantee availability. Goblin Mines requires level 5 and `quest.shenic.trial_of_lumo`; Forgotten Catacombs requires level 15 and `quest.shenic.crystal_currents`. Entry, selection and preview preserve existing previous-difficulty, Tower and other access rules. Target selection does not require possession of the entry sigil; actual entry still consumes it normally.
- Actual run creation freezes the selected target's identity, stats, item ID and acquisition terms. Switching targets during a run affects later runs; changing or clearing selection does not reset that pool's progress. Simulation does not create a commitment. Completion honors an existing commitment even after content or activation flags change.
- Only successful completion advances protection. A matching or guaranteed reward resets its pool's progress when secured, before inventory claim. Failed/retreated runs do not advance it. With no selected target the run secures only completion Scrap and neither advances nor resets progress. Other difficulties/families remain independent.
- A completion receipt keyed by character/run prevents replay from issuing rewards or advancing progress twice. Pending equipment retains its exact frozen descriptor; claims do not reroll or reevaluate it and do not reset newer progress. Missing item bases abort before currency/experience payments. Receipts survive the existing claim flow's deletion of the run.
- Recovery entitlement comes from the original durable starter grant, so losing equipment cannot block recovery behind a later quest. It restores only missing original definition/tier counts as plain rank-0, personally bound equipment, with zero salvage basis and no paid investment. Inventory, equipped items and secured unclaimed rewards count by distinct item ID; two-handed slot duplication does not count twice. A modified plain item still counts; guild-owned equipment does not.
- Recovery uses the original frozen descriptors with new item IDs and Recovery provenance. It grants no Scrap, Cinders, profession XP or generic quest loot credit. Repeating an operation ID returns the recorded result, even after recovered items are lost; a new operation can restore a newly missing baseline. Competing requests use the existing command transaction/process lock plus character row lock. Target and recovery writes invalidate dungeon or inventory/equipment state through the existing synchronization pipeline.
- Dedicated `equipment.model_e_secured` and `equipment.model_e_baseline_recovered` outbox events record committed outcomes. The player screen is connected. Recovery events do not advance quest objectives or grant generic quest loot credit.

### Acquisition API and activation

The versioned `EquipmentAcquisition` controller always uses the authenticated character:

- `GET sources`: protection pools, target previews and current eligibility/progress.
- `POST target`: `{ "poolId": "...", "definitionId": "..." }`; `null` clears the selection while preserving progress.
- `GET recovery`: original entitlements, current owned counts and missing counts.
- `POST recovery`: `{ "operationId": "...", "kind": "FirstWeapon" }` or `ReadyForRoad`. Retain the operation ID for network retries.

Completion is invoked by the trusted dungeon lifecycle, with no public reward-grant endpoint. `EquipmentProgression:ProtectedAcquisitionEnabled` and `EquipmentProgression:BaselineRecoveryEnabled` both default to `false`; no environment settings changed. The API and worker package both new catalogs with the existing starter/style/price files.

## Ordinary combat acquisition and repeat sigil access

- `equipment-ordinary.v1.json` explicitly covers the ten Shenic areas and excludes training and other regions. Each eligible victory has a provisional 0.03% chance of a uniformly selected plain Common Tier-1 rank-0 discovery across the 31 archetypes. It is unbound until personal use, has one base Scrap and no paid rank basis. Starter/protected definitions retain zero base salvage.
- After an eligible regional encounter, the player can select an exact plain target. Only victories advance it; 360 secure one bound rank-0 item, reset that progress and pause the target. Switching or clearing a target preserves partial progress. Unrelated random discoveries do not reset the elective counter. A new selection is required for another guaranteed item, including a second identical one-handed weapon.
- Selection freezes the complete target descriptor, item ID and victory threshold. Before changing either equipment or sigil selection, the command settles due combat under the old choices. A remaining catch-up backlog returns an error without changing the selection or consuming its operation ID; already earned combat rewards remain committed and refresh their state. The idle scheduler resolves encounters at their start boundary, so the new choices apply to subsequent encounters.
- A sigil selection requires the family's authored level and completed quest. Every 4,320 eligible victories awards one existing bound sigil and restarts that counter. Switching between unlocked families preserves one shared partial counter; it does not provide separate simultaneous incomes. Clearing the family pauses progress. Equipment progression Trial of Lumo and Crystal Currents now grant the first-entry sigils.
- Scrap accrues as an integer numerator in units of 1/8,640 Scrap: each victory adds the area's configured 36–54 numerator, awards whole Scrap and retains the remainder. This avoids floating-point drift and preserves fractions across claims, reloads and area changes. Losses grant no equipment, Scrap or access progress. The existing offline cap and encounter cadence are unchanged.
- A character/pool checkpoint stores the last processed schedule generation and encounter start time, including defeats. Replays, overlapping batches and older schedule generations cannot award or advance twice. Discovery rolls and item IDs use those stable boundaries rather than encounter IDs that include a batch-local sequence. Progress, inventory settlement, economy history and outbox events use the existing command transaction. No row is added for every ordinary encounter.
- Rewards feed the existing inventory/loot writer and combat summary. Individual equipment progression items retain their frozen descriptor, binding and ID even when two drops share an item base; resource totals remain compact. A secured elective target emits `equipment.model_e_plain_target_secured`. Missing reward bases or unbound sigil definitions abort the transaction instead of silently discarding rewards.
- Legacy characters retain their existing reward paths. Characters using the Forge now filter retired equipment/materials from combat and dungeon rewards and do not earn gathering rewards/XP. Essence rewards, random sigils and ordinary XP/Cinders retain their existing paths; old random-sigil income still needs a coordinated decision alongside the new elective income. These flags are not authorization for a mixed live cutover. Recovery covers original starter grants and durable earned plain-target entitlements.
