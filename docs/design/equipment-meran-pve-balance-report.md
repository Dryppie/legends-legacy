# Meran PvE balance assessment

Updated: 3 September 2026.

The original assessment evaluated 270,336 fights against actual Meran area spawns and dungeon rosters with the production combat rules. It found a pronounced Tangled Cave III spike; that authored enemy multiplier changed from 1.3 to 1.1 after matched and independent-seed checks.

The encounter findings remain relevant to the authored equipment states used by the simulation. Previous Scrap supply, Cinder upgrade cost, rank-purchase, style-change, salvage, and Forge pacing conclusions are obsolete because the equipment Forge has been removed.

Further balance acceptance should cover complete dungeon runs, broader Essence and counter-build progression, and authenticated gameplay after pending migrations are applied locally. Upgrade pacing must wait for the replacement equipment-upgrade design.

No database update or deployment was performed as part of the assessment or the Forge removal.
