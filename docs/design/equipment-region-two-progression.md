# Meran / Tier 2 equipment progression

Updated: 3 September 2026.

Meran extends the equipment drop model with four ordinary areas, Tier 2 plain choices, repeat sigils, six protected dungeon pools, named identities, authored styles, and tier-aware recovery.

Ordinary selection progress is scoped by pool so Shenic and Meran choices cannot collide. Dungeon target commitments freeze the selected Tier 2 descriptor and protection terms. Recovery compares both definition and tier; a Tier 1 holding does not satisfy a missing Tier 2 entitlement.

Meran gear keeps its authored rank and style for deterministic evaluation, but the player cannot mutate those values. The previous Forge pricing, rank/style mutation, salvage, and Blueprint reward work has been removed. The separate `ScopeOrdinaryEquipmentSelectionsByPool` migration remains required for current pool-scoped selection receipts.

The transition and combat reports remain useful as historical balance evidence for the authored gear states they evaluated. Their Scrap-income and Forge-pacing projections are obsolete.
