# Meran / Tier 2 equipment progression

Updated: 5 September 2026.

Meran extends the equipment drop model with four combat areas, Tier 2 random equipment, regional Sigils, and random dungeon equipment.

Every Meran combat area can drop any equipment archetype as Tier 2, rank 0 gear. Equipment has a 1/864 chance per victory (about 10 pieces per 24 hours of continuous wins at the 10-second cadence); rarity is Common 85%, Uncommon 12%, or Rare 3%. Each victorious encounter also has a 1/4,320 chance to drop either a Tangled Cave or Great Tree Sigil. Completed Meran dungeons have a 50% base equipment chance plus 5 percentage points per shared mastery level (up to 100%), use difficulty-specific rarity odds, and award Tier 2, rank 1 gear.

Both sources use the former Mastery 100 quality odds (35% Standard, 45% Fine, 16% Exceptional, and 4% Masterpiece; Crude remains a supported authored quality at 0% natural drop chance) and a frozen ±5% attribute-budget roll.

Meran gear keeps its awarded rarity, rank, and style for deterministic evaluation, but the player cannot mutate those values. The previous targeting, Forge pricing, rank/style mutation, salvage, and Blueprint reward work has been removed.

The transition and combat reports remain useful as historical balance evidence for the authored gear states they evaluated. Their Scrap-income and Forge-pacing projections are obsolete.
