# Possible Combat Styles

Six additional concepts to explore alongside Bastion, Conduit, Reaper, Shepherd, and Gambler. Duelist and Spellweaver now have full design proposals; the other four remain early ideas. None is implemented or a committed design. Each would operate automatically through the existing Combat Style slot.

## Duelist

**Purpose:** Give sustained single-target builds a style centered on learning and overcoming one opponent.

**What it might do:** Repeated attacks against the same enemy could reveal an opening that a later Essence attack exploits for a stronger strike. Changing targets would begin a new read on the opponent, making focused pressure and reliable target selection central to the build.

**Full proposal:** [Duelist](styles/duelist.md) develops this into Read and Opening, with mastery progression, three refinements, upgrades, an Opening Technique and worked examples.

## Avenger

**Purpose:** Connect durability and recovery with an aggressive retaliation playstyle.

**What it might do:** Health actually lost to enemy damage could build Resolve. A later offensive Essence could spend that Resolve on a retaliatory strike, turning surviving a dangerous exchange into an opportunity to push back. Recovery would help sustain that pattern over a longer battle.

## Spellweaver

**Purpose:** Encourage builds that combine Physical and Magical Essences into a complementary rhythm.

**What it might do:** A Physical Essence activation could prepare a benefit for a subsequent Magical activation, and vice versa. Weaving between the two could strengthen their effects or expose the enemy to the other damage channel. The interaction would happen between separate activations, so a single mixed or multi-hit ability would not weave with itself.

**Full proposal:** [Spellweaver](styles/spellweaver.md) develops this into an alternating cast pattern, with mastery progression, three refinements, upgrades, an Opening Technique and worked examples.

## Saboteur

**Purpose:** Make maintaining hindering conditions a central build strategy.

**What it might do:** Direct Essence hits against an enemy affected by your own debuffs could reinforce one of those debuffs, such as extending its remaining duration or renewing part of its effect. Combining different hindering conditions could create further opportunities to disrupt the enemy's next ability. This would connect offensive Essences with sustained control and weakening effects.

## Echo

**Purpose:** Give builds a delayed second wave of damage, recovery, or protection.

**What it might do:** An eligible Essence effect could leave an echo that repeats a smaller portion of that effect after a delay. A powerful attack might leave a second impact, while a healing or Barrier effect could provide follow-up support. Echoes would not generate further echoes, keeping the identity centered on a delayed follow-up.

## Tactician

**Purpose:** Make enemy attention and changes in combat roles matter to the character's build.

**What it might do:** Drawing enemy attention could prepare a defensive response, while an enemy turning toward an ally could prepare an offensive opportunity against it. Threat management, summons, and teammates would influence when the character protects themselves and when they exploit an opening. These responses would follow the battle automatically.
