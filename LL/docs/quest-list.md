# Quest List

Updated: 4 September 2026. The current catalog contains 28 regular quests. See the [quest flow guide](../../LEGENDSLEGACY_QUEST_FLOW.md) for unlocks, rewards, event status and saved-progress handling.

## Implemented Quests

Requirements below are the objectives that must be completed, not the prerequisites that unlock the quest.

### Tutorial

- [x] **Your First Hunt** — choose Goblin Warrior, Hollow Stag, or Skeleton, then defeat the chosen creature in the Training Area.
- [x] **The Soul Archive** — absorb the chosen First Hunt Essence, then equip any Essence in a loadout. Existing ownership and loadouts count automatically; completion grants an Arms Chest.
- [x] **First Weapon** — open the Arms Chest, choose one of five one-handed weapons, and equip it.
- [x] **Into the Ruins** — win one encounter in Lumo Ruins.

### Shenic Campaign

- [x] **Trial of Lumo** — win 4 encounters in Lumo Ruins and reach level 5.
- [x] **Blood in the Grove** — win 4 Blood Grove encounters and reach level 10.
- [x] **Crystal Currents** — win 5 Crystal Creek encounters and reach level 15.
- [x] **The Restless Dead** — earn and equip a plain-target archetype, win 5 Moonlit Graves encounters and reach level 20.
- [x] **Between Day and Night** — win 5 Twilight Clearing encounters and reach level 25; receive a Goblin Sigil.
- [x] **The Roots Remember** — win 6 Old Forest encounters, clear Goblin Mines I and reach level 30.
- [x] **Heart of the Hollow** — win 6 Thornroot Hollow encounters, then clear Forgotten Catacombs I.
- [x] **Ash Beneath the Earth** — win 12 Embercap Burrows encounters and reach level 40.
- [x] **The Veil Over the Marsh** — win 15 Moonveil Marsh encounters and reach level 45.
- [x] **Last Light in Duskmire** — win 20 encounters in Duskmire Hollow.

### Other Quests

Crafting, gathering and Potential-based tempering quests have been removed. These 14 side quests remain:

- [x] **A Second Soul** — own two distinct Essences in the Soul Archive; existing collections count.
- [x] **An Adaptable Archive** — attune Goblin, Lumo Wisp, and Lumo Sentinel Essences in sequence.
- [x] **Focused Pursuit** — receive an Essence drop from the creature currently set as your Essence Focus.
- [x] **The Arena Calls** — start one Colosseum battle; winning is not required.
- [x] **An Omen Fulfilled** — complete one daily Prophecy.
- [x] **Blood Grove Veteran** — win 25 encounters in Blood Grove.
- [x] **A Name in Shenic** — reach character level 10.
- [x] **Tested Wanderer** — reach character level 25.
- [x] **Warden of Shenic** — win one encounter in Duskmire Hollow as a level 45 character.
- [x] **The Archive Deepens** — ascend an Essence.
- [x] **Resonant Pair** — equip three Essences that share an ability tag other than Physical or Melee.
- [x] **Sigils in the Dust** — enter a dungeon.
- [x] **Into the Depths** — complete a dungeon run and claim its rewards.
- [x] **Tournament Tested** — battle in a tournament.

### Future Quest-System Ideas

- [ ] **Repeatable bounties** — complete a rotating combat-area, dungeon, Colosseum, or Prophecy objective before its reset.
- [ ] **Branching quests** — make a permanent choice, then complete the objectives belonging to the selected branch.
- [ ] **Account-wide quests** — complete account-level goals such as clearing regions with multiple characters, collecting titles, earning Legacy Renown, or completing achievements across categories.
