Random Pool

Builds a fixed pool of random teams using the selected participants per team and essences per participant. The battle count is then spent matching teams from that pool, so each ranked combination can collect repeat fights.

Saved vs Saved

Uses saved combinations as the candidate list and runs every saved team against every other saved team. The battle count is applied per pairing, with sides alternated to reduce side bias.

Reading Results

Win rate is calculated from that combination's fights only. Higher fight counts are more trustworthy. Damage and ticks are averages across all fights for the listed combination.
