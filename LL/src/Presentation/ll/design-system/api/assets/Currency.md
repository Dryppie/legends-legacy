## Usage notes

# Currency

Full-colour art, for display: the CurrencyPill in the TopBar and shop headers, and reward reveals, at the CurrencyPill's size and up.

- `Cinders.webp` — Cinders (the soft currency). A burning ember shard in a cracked, dark-iron setting.
- `Soulstones.webp` — Soulstones (the premium currency). A cut cyan crystal shard.

Both are 128px WebP with transparency, the game's own art (`src/assets/game-emblems/`, which also has 64 and 256px sizes); they replaced `Coins.svg` and `Diamonds.svg` (D-126).

Inline — cost lists, Ledger rows, reward lines and running text — each resource takes its one-colour line icon at 16px instead: `cinders` (to be drawn) and `soulstones` (the sidebar's gem). The art is never shrunk into a cost line (Foundations · Iconography · Resource, Registries · Resources).
