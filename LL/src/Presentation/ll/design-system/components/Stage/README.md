# Stage

The scene backdrop.

**Status:** Draft

The art-filled centre of a screen; everything the player is looking at sits on it.

**Provide:** `image` (a Backgrounds or Cards asset URL), optional `focus` (background-position) and overlay `children`.

- The art is darkened, warmed and slightly blurred, then covered by a `ground-deep` vignette, a fade to `ground` at the foot and a film grain — the art is atmosphere, never content. The veil and the grain belong to the art: a Stage without an `image` has neither (Foundations · Ornament).
- Paragraphs never sit straight on the art: lore goes in a Panel, which is its contrast surface. Only short labels sit on the art.
- Any text set directly on the art uses `shadow-text-art` as its text-shadow.
