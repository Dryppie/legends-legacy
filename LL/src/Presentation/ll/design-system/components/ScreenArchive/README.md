# ScreenArchive

The showcase for ArchetypeArchive: the Creature Archive.

**Status:** Draft

The Creature Archive: tabs and filters across the top, a fading list of creatures over the scene, a lore panel, and the selected creature's stats in the Folio.

**Composed of:** GameShell · NavRail (Essences active) · Chronicle (chat layout Docked, collapsed to its ticker under the Folio at 1280px) · TopBar · Stage (Cards/combatArea.webp) · TabStrip ×2 · EntryList · Panel (lore) · Folio `align="start"` with Heading + `titleSub`, LevelPlate (essence progress), Meter, SectionRule bands, StatTiles in `.lg-statgrid`, ItemSlot.

Click a name to change the detail. Stats are generated sample values; creature and region names are the game's.
