# NumeralSpecimen

The numeral rules in use.

**Status:** Draft

Foundations · Numerals, checked against real Legend's Legacy numbers:

- **Signs.** The true minus, the en-dash range, the × multiplier, the spaced slash, the em dash for unknown, 0 for zero, and a unit after its number.
- **Attributes.** A Ledger of mixed precision — 1,284; 24.8%; 84 HP/5s; 184.6 threat/s; 1.12; ×1.5; 0%; — — aligned on the decimal point, with units smaller and muted.
- **Bazaar prices.** Sell orders for Dire Wolf Essence: right-aligned quantity, price and total columns, the currency named once in the caption, and today's range.
- **Changes.** An equipment comparison with Now, Change and After columns: deltas with their glyph and sign, and before and after values aligned on the decimal point.
- **Live values.** A CurrencyPill, an HP Meter, a damage counter and an auction bid that update every 0.7 seconds. Each reserves its width, so nothing beside it moves. A "don't" row shows the same bid without reserved width.
- **Order by context.** The number-and-label order in the TopBar, a cost list, a table, a tooltip and a Ledger.

Only Barlow Condensed and Barlow set numbers here; Marcellus numerals are kept for the StatFigure and the LevelPlate.
